# How Black Programs Recruit New Engineers

[00:00] There has to be turnover. Let's just think about this economically. If there's a reverse engineering program or any program where anybody is building alien technology, let's just call it that, sometimes those people die. Sometimes those people retire. You have to have new blood coming into the game and you have to think about this. So how do you do it? Well, I would do it exactly like Bob Lazar's story in S4. I would have a situation where I send people out to the desert somewhere. I would show them some older prototypes of the technology so they can see what the technology is. So they can, in their mind, try to figure out how it works. And the ones that don't have a psychological breakdown, the ones that have aptitude to understanding the technology, I bring those people in. I say, okay, you're part of the team. Will you shut the hell up and never talk about this? Great. Well, you're going to be a millionaire, maybe a billionaire then.
