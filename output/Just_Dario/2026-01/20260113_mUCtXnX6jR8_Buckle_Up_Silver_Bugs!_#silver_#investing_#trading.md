# Buckle Up Silver Bugs! #silver #investing #trading

[00:00] For a Gamma Squeeze to be effective, you need two elements.
 The first element is a significant amount of open interest around at the money or slightly
 in the money options.
 And then you start buying in size out of the money call options in order to force dealers
 to etch the delta for those call options.
 But once they etch the delta, effectively they fabricate buying volume on the underlying
 asset in the market.
 Consequently, the price of the underlying is pushed higher.
 But raising the price of the underlying significantly increases the amount of delta that market makers
 will have to etch for options that are at the money or becoming deeper in the money because
 they were at the money slightly before.
 If you do this very quickly and in significant sizes, the ultimate effort can be an incredible
 spike in the underlying prices.
 And contrary to what a normal market function will dictate, it will also increase the volatility
 on the way up because of the very sustained bid on out of the money options.
 And on the other side, market makers will start increasing the implied volatility on those
 options in order to create a buffer for them that they will use to manage the risk in etching
 option options activity.
 Now, let me add one more element here.
 What happens if you have a situation when you know that a specific asset is being heavily
 shorted and then you start triggering a powerful gamma squeeze?
 The combined effect of the two can quickly unfold into a spectacular short squeeze.
 Because you will see the underlying price of the asset quickly rising, starting to trigger
 margin calls on those shorts.
 Those shorts will then be forced either to post margin, but more often than not, to close
 their position and close them quickly.
 By closing those positions, it means that they will have to buy the underlying in the market.
 But again, if they buy the underlying in the market while a gamma squeeze is unfolding,
 then you have a snowballing upward volume on the underlying that will bring options more and
 more deep into the money, hence forcing dealers to hedge a higher and higher delta.
 And all of what has been described so far is currently converging on silver exactly next
 week, potentially starting as soon as today.
