 What exactly does this mean?

 And then you know that if the price of the underlying asset and the price of the contract moves against you to that specific dollar level that you posted as margin, you will face a margin call.
 And when the margin call strikes, you already know in advance the fixed dollar amount that you are going to post.
 This was the same for silver futures and precious metals margin calculation before Tuesday.
 After Tuesday this week, the game changed.
 Now, when you want to open a contract at a specific price, yes, you know what the initial margin requirement is that you have to post, because it's a percentage of the notion.
