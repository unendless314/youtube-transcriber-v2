# Easter Island's Ancient Megaliths: Lost Civilization?

[00:00] The intriguing thing about Easter Island is there is suggestions of a pre-existing megalithic civilization that the ancestors of Alejandro went on to emulate. There are megalithic blocks here that intriguingly are connected and shaped very similarly to the ones that we've seen in Peru and Bolivia. Polygonal blocks perfectly cut out of very solid stone.
