# Remember when the news actually informed you?

[00:00] They went from being newscasters who were delivering information to delivering opinions that would inflame you.
 And that was for a purpose.
 You can remember Walter Cronkite as I was growing up.
 The evening news was 30 minutes.
 They had news bureaus all over the nation and all over the world.
 And they would go out and do news reports about what had happened.
 And then within that news story, they would get one side and have an expert give his opinion.
 And then they'd balance that out with someone with a contrary opinion.
 They made sure that you got information so that you stayed in formation.
 They wanted society to conform to be in a certain formation.
 With the advent of cable news, they had a lot of time to fill.
 They realized that money saver, they could just pay somebody to bloviate on set.
 And so they would sit there and talk.
 Over time, it was allowed to have their opinions creep in.
