 2026 UAP Detection and Tracking Summit. I'll be wearing a couple of different hats at this event.

 focuses on unidentified submerged objects or USOs, which I think remain one of the most important
 and least understood aspects of the entire UAP mystery. I won't just be talking about isolated
 sightings here. I will be talking about repeated patterns, objects entering and exiting the ocean
 or lingering underwater or operating in ways that appear deliberate and controlled. My goal is to
 step back and ask a simple but serious question, which is what does the behavior itself tell us?
 Not speculation here or anything else other than what we can reasonably infer from decades of cases
 and military encounters as well. I'll also be moderating a session with Dr. Gary Nolan, who will
 be discussing the Skywatcher Discovery Framework. This gets into how real world field investigations are
 being done right now. Sensors, data collection, analysis, and how you move from raw observations to something that
 actually resembles evidence or science. Gary has been right at the center of that effort and I'm very confident
 this will be a very grounded discussion. Another panel I'll moderate looks at UAP detection and tracking from an
 international perspective. This matters. This is very important to me. I think different countries will see different things.
 They're going to use different systems and interpret data in different ways.
 So you need that broader view. The summit also features researchers like Dr. Beatriz Villaruel,
 whose work with historical astronomical data has raised some very genuinely provocative
 questions about long-term anomalies near Earth. There are actually quite a few presenters at this event
 who have their own unique perspective on this, all very scientifically oriented.
 And I just think like if you're interested in moving past surface level arguments and into serious observation
 detection and analysis, I think you'll find this summit worth your time. I hope to see you there.
