[00:02] The most important thing to know is that you cannot prompt Claude Cowork like you would prompt Claude Chat. With Claude Chat, you use task-first language, meaning you're guiding the conversation every step of the way. Do this first, now format it like that, now try a different approach. With Claude Cowork, you use outcome-first language, meaning you describe the end result and constraints and let Cowork figure out the approach.
