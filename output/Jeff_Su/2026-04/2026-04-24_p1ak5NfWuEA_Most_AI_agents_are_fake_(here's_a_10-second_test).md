# Most AI agents are fake (here's a 10-second test)

[00:00] With everyone calling everything an AI agent, here's a 10-second test to see if you're being lied to. A report from Gartner found that out of thousands of companies claiming to build AI agents, only around 130 are actually building real ones. So first, let's take a step back. There are really just three levels to this. Level one, Gmail's spam filter is technically AI-enhanced, but according to their own blog, it just classifies your emails as spam or not spam. This is simple classification like true or false. So it's not even generative AI. Level two, Google Docs help me write feature generates natural language text. So that is generative AI. Level three, Google's anti-gravity lets you tell an agent to build a website and the agent will write code and iterate autonomously until it produces something close to your original design, meaning it's a true agent. The takeaway here is a lot of what's being marketed as agentic products is really just stuck at level one or two.
[00:55] so don't be fooled.
