# #claude Chat vs. Cowork: Biggest 3 differences

[00:00] If you're using Cloud Chat every day, Cloud Cowork is your next level up, and here are the three biggest differences you need to know. First, chat limits you to 20 files per conversation and 30 megabytes per file, since the files need to be uploaded onto the cloud. Cowork, on the other hand, reads directly from your computer, so there's no file limit and no size cap. Second, chat has a much smaller context window, meaning it'll start compacting your conversation and losing important details way sooner than Cowork. Third, chat gives you a response in a chat window, so you still have to do something with it yourself. Cowork delivers ready-to-use files directly in your folder. For example, it took 100 plus receipts and generated an expense report in Excel and even flagged rows for me to review.
