# When to use #ChatGPT, #Gemini, and #Claude

[00:00] I use ChachiPT, Gemini, and Claude almost every day, and each one has a specific superpower.
[00:05] ChachiPT's superpower is obedience.
[00:07] When you hand it a complex checklist with a dozen requirements, it follows every single one.
[00:12] Other models might be just as smart, but give them a long set of instructions and they might quietly skip a few steps.
[00:19] Gemini's superpower is multi-modality.
[00:21] It can natively process video, audio, images, and text all together.
[00:25] So if you have a video recording, a slide deck, and a photo of a whiteboard, Gemini is the only tool that can synthesize all three in one go.
[00:33] Claude's superpower is first draft quality, meaning its first attempt is usually closer to done, especially for coding and polishing copy.
[00:41] Once you share examples of your existing work, it replicates your tone almost perfectly.
[00:46] For me personally, ChachiPT and Gemini handle the beginning of my work, ideation, research, and drafting.
[00:51] Claude handles the last mile, turning that rough output into something polished.
