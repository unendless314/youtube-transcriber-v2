# The 30-second fix for #AI on mobile

[00:00] So I think we all know we should be using AI more,
 but a lot of times AI feels like an extra step
 when it should be a seamless step.
 Here's a simple trick to fix on your phone.
 This works for iPhone and Android.
 If you're on iPhone, go to any app with a share button.
 I'll just use files.
 Tap share, scroll all the way to the right,
 tap more, and then tap edit.
 Toggle on your AI app like ChatGPT,
 drag it next to AirDrop, and hit done.
 If you're on Android, it's even easier.
 Open any file, tap share,
 then long press your AI app again
 to pin it to the top of the system share sheet.
 Now when you're browsing articles,
 you can share them to AI instantly
 since those apps are now front and center.
