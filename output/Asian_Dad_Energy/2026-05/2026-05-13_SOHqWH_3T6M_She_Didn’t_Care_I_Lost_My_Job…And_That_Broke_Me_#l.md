# She Didn’t Care I Lost My Job…And That Broke Me #layoffs #unemployed #fatherdaughter #daddydaughter

[00:00] But my little girl? My little girl doesn't care how important I am. She doesn't care about my title, my salary. She doesn't care if the world respects me or not. I bring her a tiny sticker from the grocery store and she treasures it. I buy her a cheap little hair clip and she lights up as if I had gotten her the moon. Sometimes I don't even need to buy anything at all. Just sitting besides her for a few minutes, drawing with crayons with her something silly, letting her climb to the top of my shoulders and my head. That alone sometimes make her whole day. And in return, she gives me something that no money on earth can buy. She makes me feel cherished, not admired for some imagined success, not tolerated because I'm too useful to get rid of, but deeply, innocently, purely loved for just being me.
