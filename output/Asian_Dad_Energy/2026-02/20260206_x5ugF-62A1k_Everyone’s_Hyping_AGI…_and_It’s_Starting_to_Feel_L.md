# Everyone’s Hyping AGI… and It’s Starting to Feel Like 1999  #bigtech #agi #artificialintelligence

[00:00] Now there is a lot of AI hype going on, right? You have your world models, you have your generalized
 humanoid robots coming online, you have this short and narrow path being pronounced by some
 towards Artificial General Intelligence or AGI. In my opinion, these are all either work-in-progress
 items or flat-out science fiction that is unlikely to be realized in the extreme near future.
 If anyone still remembers the internet bubble of the 2000s, that was a time where every possible
 internet idea got funding, at least for a while. But out of all of those ideas, only a few of them
 ever made money. In my mind, the AI bubble is sort of like the internet bubble, except it's orders of magnitudes bigger.
