# How to Stay Sane in Late-Stage Capitalism  #bigtech #layoffs #softwareengineer #latestagecapitalism

[00:00] Having a high savings rate, while not at all flashy, allows you to build up a large emergency fund. This mitigates the worst effects of losing your job. Maintaining a high savings rate over long periods of time allows you to take those savings and put it towards productive revenue generating investments. If you do that enough, you will build up your investments such that you're able to achieve financial independence and retire early, if you so wish.
