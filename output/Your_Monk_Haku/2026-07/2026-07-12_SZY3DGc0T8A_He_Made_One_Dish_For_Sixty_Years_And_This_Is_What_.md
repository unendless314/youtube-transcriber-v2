# He Made One Dish For Sixty Years And This Is What He Said

[00:00] I once met a man who spent over 60 years making a single kind of food.
[00:03] Not a menu of accomplishments, one dish, refined daily, for six decades.
[00:08] I asked him if he ever grew tired of making the same thing.
[00:12] He looked at me the way you look at a child who still has much to learn and said, I am not making the same thing, I am making it better than yesterday.
[00:20] The craftsman does not ask what is the least I can do to be considered accomplished.
[00:24] He asks every single day, what is the smallest correction I can make right now?
