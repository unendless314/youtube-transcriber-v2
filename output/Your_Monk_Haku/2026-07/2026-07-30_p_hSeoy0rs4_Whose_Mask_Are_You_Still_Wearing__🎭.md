# Whose Mask Are You Still Wearing? 🎭

[00:00] There is a question I want to ask you and I want you to resist answering it too quickly. What did you have to become as a child to be loved? Some of you learned to be quiet so a fragile house would not shake. Some of you learned to be impressive because stillness alone was never enough to be seen. Some of you learned to be helpful because a child who needs nothing is a child who cannot be turned away.
[00:23] Whatever the shape was, you were not lying when you built it. You were surviving. But here is the line I want you to keep. You have been performing so long, you no longer remember it is a performance. The mask kept you safe once. It is not your face. It was never your face. And setting it down is not a loss. It is only the moment you stop needing a performance to finally be seen, even by yourself. Whose face are you still wearing?
