# The Hidden Cost Of Pride

[00:00] Pride is a powerful force. It can push us to achieve, to stand tall, to protect what matters, but pride has a hidden cost. When it hardens, it becomes a wall, a wall that keeps us from apologizing, from listening, from growing. We cling to being right even when it steals our peace.
[00:19] We defend our image even when it damages our relationships. The danger isn't pride itself, it's the fear beneath it, fear of being seen as weak, fear of being wrong, fear of being vulnerable.
[00:31] But vulnerability is not defeat, it's the doorway to connection. When we let pride soften, we gain something far greater than the illusion of control. We gain clarity, humility, and the freedom to change. So the real strength is not in holding tight, but in letting go. The moment we choose honesty over ego, we reclaim our ability to learn, to heal, and to move forward with integrity.
