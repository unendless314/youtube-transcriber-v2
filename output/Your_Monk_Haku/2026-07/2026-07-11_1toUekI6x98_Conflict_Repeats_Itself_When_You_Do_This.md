# Conflict Repeats Itself When You Do This

[00:00] Conflict repeats itself when we keep reacting the same way. Anger sparks anger. Silence breeds silence. Blame fuels blame. And before we know it, we're trapped in a cycle that feels impossible to escape. A peace mantra breaks that cycle. It's not magic words. It's a reminder to pause, to breathe, to choose differently. When you repeat a mantra of peace, you interrupt the automatic reaction, you stop the chain before it continues. Instead of shouting back, you listen. Instead of holding a grudge, you release. Instead of escalating, you ground yourself.
[00:40] That's how cycles end, not by winning the fight, but by refusing to feed it. Peace isn't weakness, it's strength. It takes more courage to stop the fire than to throw fuel on it. And when you choose peace, you don't just heal yourself. You change the pattern for everyone around you.
