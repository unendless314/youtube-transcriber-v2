[00:02] We wake up asking, what can I do?
[00:04] How can I be useful?

[00:15] Every moment of stillness feels like wasted time.
[00:18] Every hour of relaxation brings a wave of guilt.
[00:22] Your need to be useful blocks your path to peace.
[00:25] It can't be earned through a to-do list.
[00:27] You don't have to justify your existence.
[00:30] Like a tree or a river, your value is inherent.
