# Non Attachment Does Not Mean Loving Someone Less

[00:00] For years, I avoided teaching this word, non-attachment, because it sounds cold, it sounds like walking away, like loving someone less.
[00:09] It's the opposite. Attachment grips the outcome. Love doesn't need to. Water never holds the riverbank, and it never once leaves it either.
[00:18] The tighter you hold something, the more it slips through your fingers. Loosen the grip, and it often stays.
[00:26] This is not about caring less. It's about caring without fear. Full teaching is on the channel now.
