# How Much of Your Pain Is Actually Yours

[00:00] There's a kind of pain that doesn't come from what's happening right now.
[00:03] It's a quiet wait, like borrowed armor you forgot you were wearing.

[00:30] That isn't a flaw.

[00:44] It's just seeing these patterns clearly, without shame, and realizing they don't belong to you anymore.
