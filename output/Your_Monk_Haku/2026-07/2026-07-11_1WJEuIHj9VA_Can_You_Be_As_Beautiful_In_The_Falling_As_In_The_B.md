# Can You Be As Beautiful In The Falling As In The Blooming

[00:00] The cherry blossom does not bloom for a season. At its peak, it blooms for perhaps a week, sometimes less. And every year, without complaint, the blossoms fall while they are still beautiful.
[00:10] Not after they have withered, not after they have become something we are relieved to see go.
[00:15] They fall at the height of their beauty. We are, all of us, more like the cherry blossom than we would like to admit. We spend our lives trying to build something that will not fall.
[00:25] But the question is not, how do I prevent the fall? The question is, can I be as beautiful in the falling as I was in the blooming?
