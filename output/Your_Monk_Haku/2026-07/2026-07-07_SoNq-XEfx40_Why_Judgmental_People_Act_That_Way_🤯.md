# Why Judgmental People Act That Way 🤯

[00:00] think about the most judgmental people you know. Their criticism usually doesn't come from a place of malice, it comes from their own insecurities. True confidence works the exact opposite way.
[00:12] The more secure you are in your own skin, the less you feel the need to judge the people around you.
[00:17] When we bring this confidence into our parenting, everything changes. If our kids can grow up without the heavy weight of our judgment, they are already deeply blessed. [00:33] Instead, they get a rare and beautiful gift, the breathing room to discover who they actually are and the freedom to grow into exactly who they were meant to be.
