# Feel Everything Without Attaching

[00:00] Every morning, life hands you a brand new ocean. Some days it's calm. Some days it's wild. But here's the truth you need to carry into today. Courage isn't about avoiding the waves. It's about learning how to feel them without letting them pull you under.
[00:15] Most people wake up already bracing themselves, already tense, already afraid of what they might feel. But monks teach something different. Your emotions are not enemies. They're signals. They're teachers. They're tides that rise and fall. Never permanent, never final.
[00:33] This morning, give yourself permission to feel everything. The excitement, the fear, the uncertainty, the hope. Feel it fully, but don't attach to it. Let it move through you like water passing through open hands. You are not the wave. You are the ocean that holds it.
[00:50] When pressure shows up today, breathe. When doubt whispers, observe it. When joy appears, let it expand. You don't drown by feeling too much. You drown by fighting what you feel.
[01:03] Your strength today is not in being perfect. It's in being present. It's in meeting every emotion with awareness instead of panic. It's in trusting that whatever rises in you will also fall.
[01:16] So step into this morning with courage. Let the waves come. Let them teach you. Let them pass. You are built to feel deeply and rise anyway.
