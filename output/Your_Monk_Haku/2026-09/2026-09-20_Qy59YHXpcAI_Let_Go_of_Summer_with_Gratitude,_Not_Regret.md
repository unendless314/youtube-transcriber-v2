# Let Go of Summer with Gratitude, Not Regret

[00:00] This is the last weekend of summer. Don't let it leave without teaching you one thing.
[00:05] Look back at the last few months. Think of the warm evenings, the plans you made, the things you worried about, and the moments that slipped by while you were busy looking ahead.
[00:15] Summer didn't ask for your permission to end, it simply ripened, faded, and began to make space for autumn. The greatest lesson this season leaves behind is the art of impermanence. We spend so much of our lives waiting for the perfect season, the moment when everything settles down, our goals are met, and life becomes easy. But nature reminds us that no season stays forever and no season is meant to be held onto. The warmth fades, the leaves turn, and the world moves on whether we are ready or not. The secret to peace isn't clinging to the sunshine or dreading the cold, it's learning to fully arrive in whichever season you are in right now.
[00:56] Before this weekend ends, step outside. Feel the autumn shift in the air, take a deep breath, and release whatever you've been holding onto from these past few months. Let summer go with gratitude, not regret, and trust that whatever comes next has its own quiet beauty waiting for you.
