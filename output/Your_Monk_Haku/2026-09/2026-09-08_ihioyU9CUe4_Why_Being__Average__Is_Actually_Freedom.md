# Why Being "Average" Is Actually Freedom

[00:00] we live in a culture that is relentlessly obsessed with being extraordinary from the moment we open our phones we are flooded with highlights of extreme success overnight success stories and the implicit message that if you aren't disrupting an industry building a personal brand or standing out from the crowd you're somehow failing we've been taught to fear the average but there is profound quiet wisdom in embracing it when we constantly strive to be special life becomes a high-stakes performance every action is measured by how impressive it looks to others turning simple moments into proof of our worth expecting ourselves to be extraordinary in everything creates an exhausting burden of perfectionism and anxiety the wisdom of being average isn't about giving up or lacking ambition it's about recognizing that the vast majority of a meaningful life happens right in the middle it's finding joy in ordinary work ordinary routines and simple human connection
[00:55] without needing a stage or an audience to validate it when you accept being average you free yourself from the pressure of constant performance you gain the freedom to try things you might fail at to enjoy hobbies without needing to turn them into side hustles and to appreciate life for what it actually is not what it looks like on a feed you don't need to be extraordinary to live a deeply full peaceful and significant life sometimes the most heroic thing you can do in a world demanding distinction is to simply be human
