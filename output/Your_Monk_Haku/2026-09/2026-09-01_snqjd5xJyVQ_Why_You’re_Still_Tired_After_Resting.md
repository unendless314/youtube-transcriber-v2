# Why You’re Still Tired After Resting

[00:00] you think you're exhausted because you finished everything. But, what if the real reason is that you never stopped long enough to continue? You know that moment when you finally sit down and instead of feeling calm, your mind keeps running. You scroll. You replay conversations. You feel guilty for resting even though your body is begging for it. It's strange. You're still tired, even after taking a break. And yet, here's the part we rarely notice. Your mind doesn't treat rest as rest when it feels like you're stealing it. It treats it like a pause you haven't earned.
[00:37] The Buddha understood something simple. Suffering grows when we treat ourselves like a machine that must justify every moment of stillness. Rest is not the reward at the end of the road, it is the bridge that keeps the road from collapsing. So, ask yourself, what would your life look like if rest wasn't the last thing you allowed yourself, but the first?
