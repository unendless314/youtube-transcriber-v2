# If You Have 2 or 3 Friends, You're Richer Than You Think

[00:00] In a world that measures our worth by the size of our follower count or the length of our contact list, we often fall into the trap of believing that more is better, we mistake popularity for connection.
[00:11] But if you look back at the core teachings of the Buddha, true wealth in relationships was never about quantity, it was always about depth and quality.
[00:20] The Buddha once spoke of Kalyanamitata, which translates to spiritual, noble friendship.
[00:26] When his close disciple Ananda suggested that good friendship was half of the spiritual life, the Buddha gently corrected him.
[00:34] He said, No, Ananda, noble friendship is not half of the spiritual life, it is the entirety of the spiritual life.
[00:43] To the Buddha, a true friend is not someone who merely seeks entertainment with you, but someone who sits with you in the truth, someone who grounds you when you are lost, speaks with kindness when you are suffering, and holds up a clear mirror so you can see your highest self.
[01:01] Finding even one such person in a lifetime is rare.
[01:05] If you have two or three who truly see you, accept you, and walk alongside you with sincerity, you possess a fortune.
[01:14] If your circle is small, do not view it as a shortage.
[01:18] Recognise it as a sanctuary.
[01:20] You do not need a crowd to walk a path of peace and understanding.
[01:24] A few genuine souls are more than enough to light the way.
[01:28] If you have a few real friends, hold them close and guard those bonds with care.
[01:33] You haven't missed out on the world you've received one of its rarest gifts.
