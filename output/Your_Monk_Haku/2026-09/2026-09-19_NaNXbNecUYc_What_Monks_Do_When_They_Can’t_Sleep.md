# What Monks Do When They Can’t Sleep

[00:00] When your mind won't rest tonight, monks do this.
[00:03] They stop trying to force sleep.
[00:05] When you lie awake in the dark, watching the clock and fighting your racing thoughts, a subtle conflict begins.
[00:11] You get frustrated with your mind for being active, and that frustration creates adrenaline, making your body even more alert.
[00:18] Your bed transforms from a place of rest into a battleground of tension.
[00:23] In monastic traditions, monks understand that the mind cannot be commanded to be quiet through force.
[00:30] Trying to force a turbulent mind to be still is like trying to smooth choppy water with your hand.
[00:36] You only create more waves.
[00:39] Instead of fighting the noise, monks practice witnessing it without judgment.
[00:44] They lie still, unclench the jaw, drop the shoulders, and shift their focus away from the stories in their head and directly into the sensations of the body.
[00:55] They pay attention to the gentle rise and fall of the abdomen with each breath, or the cool feel of the air against their skin.
[01:02] They observe thoughts passing by like clouds across a night sky, noticing them but refusing to chase them down.
[01:09] If your mind refuses to sleep tonight, give up the battle.
[01:13] Step out of the story you are telling yourself and simply anchor your attention to your breath.
[01:19] Tell yourself, my body can rest even if my mind is active.
[01:24] Shift from trying to control the night to simply resting within it.
[01:27] In that quiet surrender, peace finds you.
