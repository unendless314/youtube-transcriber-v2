# Karma Isn't What You Think It Is

[00:00] We often think of karma as a dramatic cosmic hammer, a sudden lightning bolt of justice that strikes down those who hurt us or instantly rewards our good deeds. But in reality, karma rarely works with a loud crash. It operates in silence. It works in the quiet, unseen spaces of our daily lives. Karma is not a punishment system managed by the universe. It is simply the law of momentum. Every thought you dwell on, every action you take, and every word you speak is a seed dropped into the soil of your mind and environment. You might not see the sprout today or even next week, but beneath the surface, roots are forming. When you choose patience over outburst, when you act with integrity when no one is looking, or when you quietly forgive someone who never apologized, it can feel like your effort went unnoticed. But karma is already working. The immediate result of a good action isn't always a reward from the outside world. It is the peace, self-respect,
[01:03] and light you cultivate inside yourself. In the same way, those who act out of malice, greed, or deceit carry the immediate karma of their own unrest. They may seem to win in the short term, but they must live inside a mind shaped by their own hostility. Karma is the echo of your life.
[01:21] What you put into the world does not disappear into thin air. It curves back toward you, shaping your character, your relationships, and your peace of mind. Trust the quiet process. Keep planting good seeds, keep acting with a clean heart, and let time handle the harvest.
