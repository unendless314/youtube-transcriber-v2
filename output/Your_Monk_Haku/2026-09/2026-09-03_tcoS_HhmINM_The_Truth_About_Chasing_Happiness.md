# The Truth About Chasing Happiness

[00:00] you're not going to like this first sentence. You don't actually want happiness. You want the feeling you think comes right before it. You know that moment, when you're almost at the goal, almost at the promotion, almost at the version of yourself you promised you'd become.
[00:15] Your chest tightens, your mind speeds up. You tell yourself, just a little more, then I'll finally breathe. But here's the strange part. The closer you get, the more anxious you feel. It's like chasing a shadow. Every step you take makes it move. And yet, the Buddha understood something simple. Craving doesn't pull happiness toward you. It pushes it away. Because craving says, this moment isn't enough. I must escape it. Happiness isn't hiding in the next achievement. It's the quiet that appears when you stop demanding the moment to become different. The mind thinks happiness is a destination. But it's really what arrives when you stop running. If you paused right now, truly paused, what feeling would finally catch up to you?
