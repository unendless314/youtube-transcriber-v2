# The Hidden Mindset Blocking Your Wealth

[00:00] You say you want more money, but listen closely. You're really asking for permission to want more.
[00:05] Notice how your chest tightens when you imagine wealth, how your mind whispers, who do you think you are, how you shrink your dreams before anyone else can.
[00:14] The strange part is, money isn't blocking you, your old beliefs are.
[00:20] The Buddha understood this. Suffering grows when we cling to the story that we are not worthy of abundance. So repeat this gently, not as a spell, but as a truth you're finally allowing yourself to hold. I am worthy of receiving. I am allowed to expand. Say it until your nervous system believes you. Say it until your life begins to match it. What would you allow yourself to receive if you stopped apologizing for wanting more?
