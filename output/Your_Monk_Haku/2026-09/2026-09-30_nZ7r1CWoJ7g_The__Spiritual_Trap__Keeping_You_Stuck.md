# The "Spiritual Trap" Keeping You Stuck

[00:00] Enlightenment isn't a sudden lightning bolt, and chasing a spiritual high is actually what's keeping you stuck.
[00:06] Most people meditate or seek breakthrough moments to escape normal life.
[00:11] They chase rapture, deep focus, or emotional bliss.
[00:14] But when that high inevitably fades, they feel empty, trapped in a cycle of constantly hunting the next peak.
[00:21] In classical Buddhist teachings, when the monk Sariputta entered the deepest states of meditative absorption, the jhanas, he didn't lose himself in the euphoria.
[00:32] He dissected it.
[00:33] Under the microscope of his own awareness, he observed pleasure, thought, and rapture as they arose, lingered, and passed away.
[00:41] He experienced the bliss fully, but saw it for what it truly was, temporary and conditioned.
[00:48] Freedom isn't about holding on to the high.
[00:50] Real growth is the ability to enjoy the peak without clinging to it.
[00:54] So when it passes, you're free to step into what's next.
