# Try This 5-Minute Habit Before Sunday Ends

[00:00] Try this before Sunday ends.
[00:02] Find five minutes, step away from every screen in your home, and sit with a single cup of tea or water.
[00:08] Don't listen to a podcast, don't scroll through social media, don't try to plan your upcoming week or solve a single problem.
[00:15] Just sit, hold the warm cup in your hands, and give your full, undivided attention to that single moment.
[00:22] We spend so much of our weekend carrying the weight of the week that just passed, or anticipating the stress of the week that's about to begin.
[00:30] We rush through our rest just like we rush through our work.
[00:34] But when you dedicate five uninterrupted minutes to simply being right where you are, you break that cycle.
[00:39] You remind your nervous system that you are safe, that there is nowhere else you need to be, and that peace isn't something you have to earn, it's something you allow.
[00:49] Before the weekend slips away, give yourself the gift of complete presence.
[00:54] Drink your drink, take a deep breath, and let the rest of the world Y.T.
[00:59] Namaste.
