# Discipline vs. Pleasure: You’re Doing Both Wrong

[00:00] Two bowls sit on the same tray. One is bitter enough to make a grown man wince. One is sweet enough to make a child cry with joy. The same tea master hands you both at the same time, and for 400 years monks have killed careers over which one you're supposed to refuse.
[00:14] Most people think peace means giving something up. Comfort. Pleasure. Anything that feels good.
[00:19] So they empty the bowl completely and call it discipline. Others think a good life just means adding more. More comfort. More pleasure. Stacked higher and higher until the stack is tall enough to call it happiness. Both of them end up in the exact same place. Numb. Cold. Unable to taste anything at all. There's a third way, and it's older than either of the two you were taught. Watch the full sermon to see why the tea master never asks you to choose.
