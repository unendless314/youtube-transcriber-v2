# Stop Needing to See the Whole Map

[00:00] You're not lost. You're just trying to see the whole map before taking a single step.
[00:05] And that's why you feel stuck. Because every night, you lie in bed, replaying the same question.
[00:12] What am I supposed to do with my life? Your mind zooms out so far that everything becomes blurry.
[00:18] Every path looks wrong. Every choice feels permanent. But here's the strange part.
[00:24] The future isn't asking you to solve it. It's only asking you to be honest about one small truth you already feel right now. The Buddha understood this quiet mistake. Suffering grows when we demand certainty from a life that only ever gives us the next moment. Think of a lantern. It doesn't light the whole road. It only shows the next few steps. And yet, that's enough to keep walking.
[00:53] So maybe the question isn't, "What should I do with my life?" Maybe it's, "What is the next honest step I already know I'm avoiding?" Take that step. The path appears after you move.
[01:07] If you paused at that last line, ask yourself, "The next honest step you already feel?"
