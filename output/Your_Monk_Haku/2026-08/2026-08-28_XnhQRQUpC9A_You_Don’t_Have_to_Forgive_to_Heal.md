# You Don’t Have to Forgive to Heal

[00:00] You're not tired of the past, you're tired of how heavy it still feels in your chest.
[00:05] And that's strange, isn't it? Because the person who hurt you isn't here. The moment is over, yet you still feel it, like a blade you keep accidentally brushing against.
[00:16] You tell yourself you should forgive, you tell yourself you're weak for still feeling it, but here's the part your mind doesn't see. You're not holding on to the memory, you're holding on to the wound, and yet the wound isn't asking you to forgive.
[00:33] It's only asking you to stop pressing on it. The Buddha understood something simple.
[00:38] Suffering grows when we keep gripping what already cut us, like carrying a knife by the blade, hoping the pain will teach us something. You don't have to forgive today. You don't have to be wise or healed or above it. You only have to loosen your hand and let the knife rest where it belongs.
[00:57] Because the moment you stop carrying it, the past finally stops cutting you.
