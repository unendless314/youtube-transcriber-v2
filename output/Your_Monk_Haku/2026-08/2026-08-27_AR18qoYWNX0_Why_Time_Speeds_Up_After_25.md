# Why Time Speeds Up After 25

[00:00] Do you notice how the years after 25 feel like they vanish overnight?

[00:09] You wonder, did time speed up, or did you stop noticing the mornings, the small conversations, the way sunlight fell on your desk?
[00:18] The strange part is, time didn't change, your attention did.

[00:33] You stop paying attention, and when you stop paying attention, life collapses into fast forward.
[00:39] Time is not rushing past you, you are rushing past time.
[00:44] Pay attention again, watch the steam rise from your coffee, listen to the silence between words, and suddenly, the years stretch open.
