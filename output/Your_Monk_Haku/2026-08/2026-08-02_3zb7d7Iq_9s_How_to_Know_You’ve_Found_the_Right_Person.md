# How to Know You’ve Found the Right Person

[00:00] Most people think the right person is someone who completes them, fixes them, or fills the empty spaces in their life.
[00:06] But monks teach something far more grounded.
[00:08] The right person is not someone who changes your world, it's someone who helps you return to yourself.
[00:13] You know you've found the right person when your nervous system relaxes around them.
[00:17] Not because everything is perfect, but because nothing feels forced.
[00:21] Your mind isn't performing, your heart isn't hiding, your energy isn't shrinking.
[00:26] You feel safe enough to be honest and brave enough to be seen.
[00:30] The right person doesn't make your life easier, they make your life clearer.
[00:33] They don't remove your challenges, they remind you you're capable of facing them.
[00:38] They don't demand your best self, they inspire it simply by being present.
[00:42] Pay attention to the small things.
[00:44] When you speak, do they listen?
[00:46] When you fall, do they stay?
[00:48] When you dream, do they encourage?
[00:51] When you're quiet, do they understand?
[00:54] The right person expands you, not with intensity, but with consistency, not with fireworks, but with alignment.
[01:01] And here's the deepest truth.
[01:02] You know you've found the right person when being with them feels like coming home, not to their life, but to your own.
[01:09] Because the right person doesn't complete you, they walk with you while you complete yourself.
