# What Buddha Actually Taught About Awakening

[00:00] You've been searching for a breakthrough, but Buddha would tell you this.
[00:03] The real awakening begins the moment you stop escaping your own mind.
[00:07] Awakening doesn't crash into your life like thunder, it rises slowly, like dawn.
[00:12] It begins in the quiet moment when you finally sit with yourself, without distraction, without noise, without running.
[00:20] When you see your thoughts clearly, when you feel your emotions honestly.
[00:23] When you stop clinging to the stories that keep you small, this is the path Buddha taught, not perfection, not performance, presence.
[00:32] Awakening is simply returning to yourself gently, bravely, fully.
