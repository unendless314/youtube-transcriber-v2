# Yoga Isn’t About Poses — It’s About This

[00:00] You think stillness means holding a pose, but the truth is, your body can be perfectly still while your mind runs a marathon. You've felt it, lying in bed, unmoving, yet every thought racing, every regret replaying, every plan unfolding. The body is quiet, the mind is not. And yet, here is the strange part. When the mind finally stops grasping, even a single breath feels like a vast silence. This is what yoga was always pointing to, not the shape of the body, but the space inside the moment. Stillness isn't the absence of movement, it's the absence of resistance. Like water settling in a glass, clarity appears only when you stop stirring.
[00:39] True yoga begins when nothing outside you has to change, for you to rest.
