# The Power of Silence When Someone is Suffering

[00:00] You think silence is useless. And yet, it's often the only thing a grieving heart can actually hear.
[00:06] Your friend sits there, staring at a spot on the floor that means nothing to you, but everything to them. You feel that familiar panic, rising, should I say something, should I fix this, should I pull them out of it, and somehow, every sentence you rehearse feels wrong the moment it reaches your throat. Here's the strange part. Their pain isn't asking for words, it's asking for permission to exist without being rushed back into the world. Grief is like a heavy stone they're forced to carry. Most people try to lift it with advice. But presence, presence lets them set it down for a moment. The Buddha understood this quietly. Suffering softens when we stop trying to rearrange it. So sit beside them, breathe with them. Let your stillness become the room they can finally rest in. Sometimes the most healing thing you can offer is simple, a moment where they don't have to pretend they're okay.
