# Stop Setting Goals. Try Tiny Experiments Instead

[00:00] stop setting goals that trap you start testing hypotheses that free you every time you declare a grand goal whether it's building a daily morning routine or writing a book your mind creates a hidden trap it sets up a binary world where you are either a success or a failure if you slip up the ego steps in with shame you failed again you lack discipline the heavy weight of that judgment is why so many of us procrastinate before we even begin we aren't lazy we are protecting ourselves from the fear of falling short in monastic wisdom we do not march toward rigid destinations with our teeth clenched we practice gentle observation in modern terms that means shifting from a goal mindset to a hypothesis mindset a goal says i must write every morning for 30 days or i am undisciplined a hypothesis says let's test what happens if i sit with a notebook for 10 minutes before opening my phone this week a goal demands a specific outcome to protect your identity
[01:03] a hypothesis seeks only data if you miss a day with a goal you have failed if you miss a day with a hypothesis you haven't failed at all you've simply gathered information you learn that your evening routine was too late or your environment was too loud you adjust the experiment step back into curiosity and try again when you live your life as a series of tiny experiments fear vanishes you stop demanding perfection from yourself and you begin discovering what actually works with quiet unshakable ease
