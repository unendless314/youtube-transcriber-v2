# One Day Her Name Won’t Pop Up on Your Phone Anymore…

[00:00] If your phone screen lights up and you see your mother's name, stop for a second before you let out that quiet sigh. How often do we look at those incoming calls as an interruption? We think, I'm busy, or she's going to ask the same questions, or I'll just call her back later when I have time. We treat those moments like small obligations in an already crowded day.
[00:23] In Zen, we speak often of Ichigo Ichi, the understanding that this exact moment will never happen again. We move through life assuming that the people who love us will always be there at the end of the line. But the universe is in constant motion. The voice on the other end of that phone is a finite gift, borrowed time in a world where nothing remains permanent. She isn't calling you because she needs information. She is calling because in a fast, noisy, indifferent world, you are still a central part of her sanctuary. She calls just to check if you're eating well, to hear the tone of your voice, to reach out across the distance and anchor herself in your presence.
[01:07] One day, you will look at your phone, and that name won't pop up anymore. You would give everything you own just to hear her ask those repetitive questions one more time, just to hear her breathe on the line. So the next time your mom calls, don't view it as a distraction from your life.
[01:26] Realize that her call is life, offering you a rare, beautiful moment of connection. Pick up the phone, soften your voice, listen closely. It is one of the greatest blessings you will ever receive.
