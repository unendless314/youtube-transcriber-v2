# Why you should cherish this moment

[00:00] Ask yourself one question during the most ordinary moment of your day.
[00:04] What if this is the last time?
[00:07] Most of our lives are spent on autopilot.
[00:10] We drink our morning coffee while scrolling on our phones, we hug our loved ones while thinking about work, and we walk through familiar rooms without really seeing them.
[00:19] We assume there will always be another morning, another conversation, another quiet cup of tea.
[00:26] In monastic training, we learn that the greatest thief of joy isn't tragedy, it is the illusion of repetition.
[00:33] When you bring the question, what if this is the last time, into an ordinary moment, everything changes instantly.
[00:40] The warm mug in your hand isn't just routine, it becomes a rare comfort.
[00:45] The voice of a friend isn't background noise, it becomes a priceless gift.
[00:50] You stop rushing through the present to get to a future that isn't promised.
[00:55] In Japanese tea ceremonies, there is a sacred concept born from this exact realization, Ichigo Ichie, Ichi Ihue.
[01:04] The understanding that every single encounter is a once-in-a-lifetime moment that can never be repeated.
[01:12] Even if the same people meet in the same room again, the time, the air, and who you are will have changed forever.
[01:20] If you feel ready to stop rushing through your life and learn how to master the art of unrepeatable moments, I go much deeper into this philosophy in my full video on Ichigo Ichi.
[01:32] I'll leave the link pinned below so you can explore it when you're ready.
[01:35] Until then, honor this exact breath because you will never have it again.
