# The Scariest Part of a Power Outage 😳

[00:00] Imagine one ordinary evening. You reach for the tap. Nothing comes out. You try again. Still nothing. You check the other faucet. Silence. You pick up your phone to search for answers and discover that half your neighborhood has lost water pressure. No explanation yet. No timeline for repair. And in that moment, that small, quiet, completely ordinary moment, something shifts inside you. A feeling you cannot quite name. Not panic. Not anger. Something quieter than both. A sudden awareness of how completely you had assumed that water would simply always be there. You had never decided to depend on that system. You had never signed an agreement.
[00:41] Never made a choice. The dependence had simply grown around you, the way a vine grows around a fence, until the two became impossible to separate. This is what I want you to sit with today. Not fear. Not urgency. Just awareness. The things we rely on most completely are the things we think about least. And the things we think about least are the ones whose absence will surprise us most. Look at your life today with that awareness. Not to worry. Simply to see clearly what is there.
