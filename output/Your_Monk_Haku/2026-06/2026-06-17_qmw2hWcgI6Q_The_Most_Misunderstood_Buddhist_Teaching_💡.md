# The Most Misunderstood Buddhist Teaching 💡

[00:00] Non-attachment is one of the most misunderstood teachings in all of Buddhism.
[00:03] People hear it and think it means caring about nothing, feeling nothing, becoming distant from the people and things you love. That is not what it means. Non-attachment means holding life the way you hold water in open hands, not grasping, not controlling, allowing what comes to come and what goes to go, without the fist tightening around it. The closed fist loses everything it tries to keep, the open hand can actually receive. Most of us spend our lives with closed fists and we wonder why peace feels so far away. There is a full teaching on this channel that walks through exactly how to begin opening that fist, slowly, honestly, without forcing anything.
[00:43] It is there when you are ready.
