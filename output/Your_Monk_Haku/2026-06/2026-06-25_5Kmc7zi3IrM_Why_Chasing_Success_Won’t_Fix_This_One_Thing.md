# Why Chasing Success Won’t Fix This One Thing...

[00:00] We sprint toward milestones, convinced that our worth is measured by the distance we climb.
[00:05] But if you strip away the glitz of the hustle, an uncomfortable question remains: Are you chasing success because it genuinely fulfills you, or are you running a marathon to outrun the fear that you aren't enough?
[00:17] This is the "when-then" trap. We tell ourselves that when we hit the next status symbol, then we will finally be worthy of respect. But the goalposts always move.
[00:30] When validation is your fuel, no amount of achievement will ever fill the bucket.
[00:35] The shift happens when you stop building to protect an insecure ego and start building out of pure curiosity. When you operate from a foundational place of feeling inherently enough, success ceases to be a lifeline and becomes a playground.
[00:50] True freedom isn't conquering the mountain to look down at the world.
[00:54] It's realizing you never needed the climb just to prove you matter.
