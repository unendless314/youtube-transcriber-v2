# The True Beginning of Compassion ✨

[00:00] When you fail at something, what story does your mind tell first?
[00:03] For most of us, the story is moral before it is practical.
[00:06] A missed deadline becomes proof of laziness.
[00:08] A moment of anger becomes proof of a broken character.
[00:11] But imagine a different way of seeing.
[00:13] Imagine that your recurring struggle is not a moral defect at all.
[00:17] Imagine it is a wounded friend.
[00:18] A wounded friend does not need to be scolded into healing.
[00:21] A wounded friend needs someone to sit beside them and ask gently, where does it hurt?
[00:26] This is the beginning of compassion.
