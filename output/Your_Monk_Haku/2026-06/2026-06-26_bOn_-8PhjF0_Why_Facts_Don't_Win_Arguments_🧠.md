# Why Facts Don't Win Arguments 🧠

[00:00] We think arguments are one with facts and logic.
[00:02] We think if we just stack up enough pristine data, the other person will see the light and change their mind.
[00:08] But they don't. They dig in.
[00:10] And that's because we commit the single biggest mistake in human disagreement.
[00:14] We argue the point instead of addressing the threat.
[00:17] The human brain rarely processes a disagreement as a mere mismatch of data.
[00:21] It processes it as a threat to identity.
[00:24] When you tell someone they're wrong, their brain doesn't register a helpful software update.
[00:29] It registers a predator in the bushes.
[00:31] The amygdala fires up, the logical brain goes offline, and their goal shifts from finding the truth to protecting the self.
[00:38] If you want to change someone's mind, you have to decouple their ego from their position.
[00:42] Stop trying to win the point and start making it safe for them to be wrong.
