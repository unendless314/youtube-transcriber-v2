# Change This One Mindset To Stop Your Suffering 🧠

[00:00] For many years, I believed that my suffering meant I was doing something wrong.
[00:04] That pain was a sign of failure, that the people who had truly found the right path were somehow moving through life without the weight I carried.
[00:11] I watched others and told myself they must know something I did not, that their lives were smoother because they were wiser, or luckier, or simply more deserving.
[00:20] It took me a long time to understand that I was looking at the surface of things and calling it the whole truth.
[00:27] The people I admired most, the ones with a stillness in them that nothing could disturb, they had not avoided suffering.
[00:33] They had gone further into it than most people are willing to go.
[00:37] They had sat inside the discomfort long enough to stop being afraid of it, and somewhere in that sitting, they stopped seeing their pain as a punishment, and began to see it as a teacher.
[00:46] Your setbacks are not evidence that you are on the wrong path.
[00:50] They are evidence that you are on a path that is asking something real of you, and that is not a problem, that is the beginning of everything that will matter about your life.
