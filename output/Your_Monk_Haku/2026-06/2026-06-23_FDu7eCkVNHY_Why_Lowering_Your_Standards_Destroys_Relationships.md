# Why Lowering Your Standards Destroys Relationships

[00:00] Ladies and gentlemen, think about the last time you shrunk yourself to make someone else comfortable. We often think lowering our standards is an act of kindness, a way to keep the peace.
[00:09] But the truth is much harsher. Lowering your standards to accommodate someone else's feelings will only create two insecure and resentful people. You become resentful of what you gave up, and they become insecure trying to live up to a version of you that isn't real.
[00:24] It is time to choose a different path. Respect yourself. Deliver on your best self every single day and watch how the room changes. When you hold your ground and live authentically, you naturally attract the people you are meant to be with, the ones who want to rise to your level, not pull you down to theirs. Never apologize for your evolution. Life is simply too short to apologize for growing into who you think you should be. Stand tall, keep your standards high, and let the right people find you.
[00:55] Thank you.
