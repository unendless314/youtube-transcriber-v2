# How to Find Your Ikigai with the 10% Rule 🧭

[00:00] We often think of Ikigai, our reason for being, as a massive, distant peak we have to climb.
[00:05] We think it requires a grand epiphany or a total life overhaul.
[00:08] But the truest way to bring Ikigai into your reality isn't by making a massive leap, it's by taking a microstep.
[00:14] Grab a piece of paper today and look at the intersection of four things.
[00:18] What you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can get paid for.
[00:23] Don't stress if they don't perfectly align right now.
[00:26] Instead, focus on the 10% rule.
[00:30] Look at your calendar for next week, and find a way to inject just 10% more of your passion or your mission into your daily routine.
[00:37] Master a small skill, mentor someone for 30 minutes, or carve out time for a creative project.
[00:43] Ikigai isn't a final destination you suddenly arrive at, it is a living, breathing practice found in the small, deliberate choices you make every single day.
[00:53] Start small, start today, and let your purpose evolve with you.
