# The 25/75 Rule: Why You’re Always Exhausted 🔋

[00:00] Imagine looking at your calendar and realizing that three-quarters of the people you hang out with are actively draining your energy.
[00:06] In his brilliant book, Think Like a Monk, Jay Shetty introduces a powerful framework to fix this, the 25-slash-75 rule.
[00:16] The concept is beautifully simple.
[00:18] Think of your time and energy as a bank account.
[00:21] Shetty argues that we should aim to spend 75% of our time surrounded by people who uplift us, inspire us, and push us to grow.
[00:30] These are the mentors, the cheerleaders, and the friends who bring light into our lives.
[00:34] The remaining 25%? That is reserved for the people who might be negative, demanding, or exhausting, whether that's a difficult colleague, a struggling family member, or a friend going through a rough patch.
[00:46] The rule isn't about cutting people off coldly or living in a perfect bubble. It's about protection and preparation.
[00:52] When you proactively fill 75% of your life with positive energy, you build an emotional reservoir.
[00:59] You create the mental strength required to show up for that other 25% with genuine patience, empathy, and grace without letting their negativity pull you down.
[01:08] So, take a look at your inner circle this week.
[01:11] Protect your 75% so you can properly serve your 25%.
