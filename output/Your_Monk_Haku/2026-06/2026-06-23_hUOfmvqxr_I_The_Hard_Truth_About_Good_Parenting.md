# The Hard Truth About Good Parenting

[00:00] Parents often say they want to give their children a better life than they had.
[00:04] But somewhere along the way, we mistake a better life for a life completely free of pain, failure, and friction. The truth is, good parenting is also about letting your kids make the mistakes that you needed to make in order to get where you currently are.
[00:18] Think about your own greatest strengths, your resilience, your wisdom, your work ethic.
[00:23] They weren't born out of smooth sailing. They were forged in the fire of your worst missteps and toughest lessons. When we overprotect our children and shield them from every possible failure, we aren't saving them. We are robbing them of the exact experiences they need to grow, build character, and discover who they are. It is painful to watch our children stumble, but our job isn't to build a fortress around them. Our job is to be the steady ground they can return to when they fall, deliver your guidance, show them unconditional love, but step back enough to let them live their own story. Loving them means trusting them to handle the fall and trusting them to find their own way back up. Thank you.
