# Why Your Failures are Actually Preparing You 🧠

[00:00] There was once a student who came to an old monk and said, I have failed at everything I have tried to build.
[00:05] Tell me what I am doing wrong.
[00:06] The monk did not answer.
[00:08] Instead, he walked the student to the edge of a field and pointed to a farmer turning the soil with a plow.
[00:14] The earth resisted.
[00:15] The plow scraped and caught on stones.
[00:18] The farmer's arms shook with the effort of it.
[00:20] The student watched and said, That looks like very hard work.
[00:24] The monk said, Yes, and the farmer is grateful for every stone the plow finds.
[00:30] Because a field that has never been broken open cannot receive a seed.
[00:33] It looks like solid ground, but nothing grows there.
[00:37] The student was quiet for a long time.
[00:40] Then he said, So my failures are the stones.
[00:43] The monk smiled and said, No, your failures are the plow.
[00:47] You are the field.
[00:49] And you are being made ready.
