# You Weren't Born Judging Yourself 🧠

[00:00] There is a voice that speaks to you before any other voice has the chance.
[00:03] It speaks in the quiet seconds after a mistake. It tells you that you are not enough, that you should have known better, done better, been better. This voice feels ancient, as though it has always lived inside you. But it was not born with you. It was built, sentence by sentence, from every harsh word you ever believed. In Buddhist teaching, this voice is not your true nature. It is a storm crossing the sky of your mind. The sky itself has never once been damaged by the storms that cross it.
