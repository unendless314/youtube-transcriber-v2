# Buddhism Isn’t What You Think 🧠✨

[00:00] When we think of Buddhism, we often picture statues, temples, or incense, but at its core, Buddhism isn't a religion of worship. It's a practical philosophy for the mind. 2,500 years ago, a man named Siddhartha Gautama didn't claim to be a god. He was simply a human being who looked at the chaos, stress, and anxiety of life and asked, how do we find peace? His answer wasn't about blind faith. It was about awareness. Buddhism teaches us that while we cannot always control what happens to us, we can entirely control how we respond. Through mindfulness, it trains us to stop dwelling on the past or worrying about the future and to inhabit the only moment we actually have, right now. Ultimately, Buddhism is a toolkit. It's the radical reminder that true peace isn't found by changing the world, but by mastering our own minds.
