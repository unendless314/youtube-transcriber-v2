# When Love Shifts to Control…

[00:00] There was a father who loved his daughter completely.
[00:02] And that love was real.
[00:04] But over time, something in it changed.
[00:08] When she chose friends he did not approve of, he felt wounded.
[00:12] When she moved away to build her own life, he could not celebrate it.
[00:16] He told himself it was love.
[00:18] But love does not feel like a wound when someone grows.
[00:22] Here is what the Buddhist teachings show us.
[00:25] There is a moment, quiet, easy to miss, when love stops being about the other person and starts being about ourselves, about our need for them to stay, to fit, to confirm that everything we gave was worth it.
[00:39] That is the moment love becomes a cage.
[00:42] And the cruelest part is this.
[00:45] The cage is built from the most sincere materials.
[00:48] Worry, sacrifice, devotion.
[00:51] The person inside it cannot always name what is wrong.
[00:55] They only feel, slowly, that they cannot quite breathe.
[00:59] If someone you love is pulling away, before you call it betrayal, ask yourself one honest question.
[01:05] Am I loving this person, or am I loving the way this person makes me feel?
[01:10] That question will tell you everything.
