# Why You Can’t Find Inner Peace 🧘‍♂️

[00:00] Most people search for peace their entire lives and never find it, not because peace is rare, not because it is reserved for monks or mystics or people with perfect lives, but because they are looking in the wrong direction. We look outward. We rearrange our circumstances, our relationships, our routines, believing that if the outside world finally cooperates, something inside us will relax. It never does. The Buddha taught something that took me many years to truly understand. Peace is not something you find. It is something you uncover. It was never missing. It was simply buried, beneath the noise, beneath the wanting, beneath the habit of fighting everything that does not go the way we hoped.
[00:43] The full teaching is on this channel. Watch it when you are ready to stop searching outward and begin the only journey that has ever led anywhere real.
