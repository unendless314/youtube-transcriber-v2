# True Confidence vs Narcissism: Why Loud Voices Hide Weakness

[00:00] We live in a culture that often confuses loud for strong and arrogant for alpha.
 We see someone taking up all the space in a room and assume they are overflowing with self-love.
 But as Dr. Romani Durvasula points out, that's the biggest misconception of all.
 True confidence is quiet. Narcissism is deafening. Why? Because it's a mask for profound self-loathing.
 What we mistake for alpha energy is often just a desperate defense against ugly insecurities.
 A person who truly likes themselves doesn't need to project power, put others down,
 or play the victim to feel significant. When someone acts like they are above everyone else,
 they are usually running away from the feeling that they are beneath everyone.
 Let's redefine what it means to be a leader. Real strength isn't found in the loudest voice
 or the biggest ego. It's found in the person who is secure enough to be kind and grounded enough
 to be quiet. Don't be fooled by the volume. True power never needs to scream.
