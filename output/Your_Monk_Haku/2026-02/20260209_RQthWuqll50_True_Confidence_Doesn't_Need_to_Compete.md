# True Confidence Doesn't Need to Compete

[00:00] We've all heard that comparison is the thief of joy.
 But for some, it isn't just a habit, it's a prison.
 We often mistake arrogance for confidence.
 But beneath that exterior is often a person trapped in a constant state of comparison.
 It is a toxic internal checklist.
 How come they have that?
 Why am I not getting this?
 Why is their life better than mine?
 This mindset creates a life of perpetual victimhood.
 When you live solely to outshine others, you lose the ability to actually be yourself.
 You become a shadow of everyone else's achievements, driven by anxiety rather than ambition.
 True freedom isn't found in having the most.
 It's found in exiting the race altogether.
 Another person's light doesn't dim your own.
 Today, stop looking sideways at the competition and start looking inward.
 The only person you should try to be better than is the person you were yesterday.
