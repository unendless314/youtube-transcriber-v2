# Why You Don’t Need Revenge on a Narcissist

[00:00] When someone treats us with cruelty, our first instinct is often to get even.
 We want them to feel the weight of the pain they caused.
 But the most profound justice doesn't actually require your effort at all.
 There is a common misconception that people who act with toxic entitlement are "winning".
 We see the ego, but we don't see the self-loathing.
 Imagine waking up every single day in a prison of insecurity,
 chronic anxiety, and a constant burning need to outdo everyone else.
 That internal reality is, in a word, miserable.
 The truth is, you don't need to seek revenge because they have to keep being them.
 While you have the capacity to heal and find genuine joy,
 they are stuck in a cycle of anger and victimhood.
 The best way to win is to reclaim your peace.
 Your healing is the only victory that matters.
