# How You’re Accidentally Manifesting Negativity

[00:00] We often think of manifestation as a conscious act involving vision boards and affirmations, but the truth is that your mind is a broadcast tower that never shuts off.
[00:11] Most of us are accidentally manifesting negativity simply by where we choose to park our attention.
[00:18] This usually happens through the anti-goal trap. When you spend your entire morning thinking, I hope I don't get stuck in traffic, or I hope my boss isn't in a bad mood, your brain is still centering the concepts of traffic and bad moods. The mind is a magnetic processor, it focuses on the subject, often ignoring the don't or the no attached to it. Furthermore, we often mistake worrying for preparation. By constantly rehearsing future disasters or ruminating on past lights, we produce the actual chemistry of stress in our bodies. We are literally practicing being miserable before anything has even happened. This creates a feedback loop where we become so efficient at spotting threats that we become blind to opportunities. You aren't being cursed by the universe. You are simply witnessing the results of a high definition focus on what you fear. To change the output, you have to change the broadcast. Stop describing what you are trying to survive and start naming what you
[01:29] intend to build.
