# Reclaiming Your Life After a Breakup

[00:00] We've all been there. The silence after a breakup where your brain starts playing a greatest hits
 reel of your ex. You remember the sunsets, the inside jokes, and the way they made you laugh.
 But here's the thing. That reel is a lie. It's a highlight, chat, edit that conveniently cuts
 out the reasons why you aren't together anymore. When you're healing, you have to be your own
 historian. Don't just remember the flowers. Remember the reasons why the relationship didn't work.
 Focus on the incompatibility, the missed calls, or the way you felt small. Holding onto the why isn't
 being bitter, it's being honest, and honesty is what sets you free. Now take all that heavy energy
 and put it into motion. Go do the things that make you feel like you
 again. Call your friends and go for a bike ride. Feel the wind on your face, the burn in your legs,
 and the literal momentum of moving forward. You aren't losing a half. You are reclaiming your whole.
 So stop looking in the rearview mirror. The view ahead is much better,
 especially when you're peddling toward your own happiness.
