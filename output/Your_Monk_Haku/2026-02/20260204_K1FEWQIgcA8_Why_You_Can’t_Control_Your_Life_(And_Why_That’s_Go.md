# Why You Can’t Control Your Life (And Why That’s Good) #yourmonkhaku #buddhism #motivation #success

[00:00] We often spend our lives trying to outrun the tide, white-knuckling the steering wheel of a
 journey we don't fully control. But there is a profound power in letting go. Allowing the
 universe to guide you isn't about being passive. It's about alignment. It's trusting that when a
 door closes, it's a redirection. And when a path opens, it's an invitation. When you stop forcing
 outcomes, you start noticing opportunities that were previously invisible. Trust the timing.
 Lean into the flow and breathe. The universe isn't happening to you. It's unfolding for you.
