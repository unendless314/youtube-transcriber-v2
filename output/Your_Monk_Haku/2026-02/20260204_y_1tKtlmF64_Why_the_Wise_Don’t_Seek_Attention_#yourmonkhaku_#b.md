# Why the Wise Don’t Seek Attention #yourmonkhaku #buddhism #motivation #mindfulness #meditation

[00:00] You are not the main character of the universe, and life is about to prove it to you.
 We all start out with a plan.
 We have our maps drawn and a certain, perhaps misplaced, confidence that we are the masters of our own orbit.
 But if you live long enough, life will eventually pull the rug out from under you.
 It happens in the quiet moments after a failure you didn't see coming,
 or the loud ones where your best efforts simply weren't enough.
 Life strips away the ego, the titles, and the masks we wear.
 It reminds us that we are not in total control.
 But here is the secret.
 Humility isn't about being humiliated.
 It's about being grounded.
 When life humbles you, it's actually offering you a gift.
 It clears away the noise of who you thought you had to be, leaving room for who you actually are.
 It replaces arrogance with empathy.
 It teaches us that true strength isn't found in never falling, but in the grace we show while getting back up.
 So, when the universe eventually brings you to your knees, don't fight the dirt.
 Look around.
 From down there, you'll find a much clearer view of what actually matters.
