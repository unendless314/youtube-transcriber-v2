# A Message for the Burned Out

[00:00] We live in a world that treats rest like a transaction.
[00:03] A reward we only unlock after crossing off every item on a never-ending to-do list.
[00:08] We tell ourselves, I'll sit down once this is finished.

[00:18] But here's the truth.
[00:20] Rest is not a reward for a job well done.
[00:24] It is a biological and emotional necessity.
[00:28] You don't earn the right to sleep just as you don't earn the right to breathe or eat.
[00:35] When we wait until we are depleted to rest, we aren't actually resting.
[00:40] We're just recovering from burnout.
[00:42] Real rest is a form of self-respect.
[00:46] It's the act of acknowledging that your value as a human being is not tied to your output, your salary, or your hustle.
[00:56] You are worthy of stillness simply because you exist.
[00:59] So today, I challenge you to reclaim your time.
[01:02] Sit in the sun, take a nap, or stare at a wall for ten minutes.
