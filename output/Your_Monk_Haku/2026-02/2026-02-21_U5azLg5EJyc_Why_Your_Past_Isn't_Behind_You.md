# Why Your Past Isn't Behind You

[00:00] We often treat our past like a storage room we've lost the key to, a place full of things we'd rather not look at. We're told to let go and move on, as if peace is found by running away. But the truth is, your past isn't behind you, it's beneath you. It is the soil from which your current perspective grows.
[00:22] Peace isn't the absence of a messy history, it's the integration of it.
[00:30] When you stop fighting your story, you stop leaking energy into suppression.
[00:34] By acknowledging your past, the lessons learned in the burn and the resilience forged in the break, you transform old ghosts into current wisdom. You cannot be at peace with the self you are trying to ignore. Your future peace depends on your ability to look back, not with regret, but with the quiet realization that every chapter was necessary to make you someone who can finally handle the silence.
