# What is a Bodhisattva? | The Path to Enlightenment

[00:00] Most people seek enlightenment as a form of personal peace, a way to exit the chaos of the world.
[00:05] However, the Bodhisattva vow flips that script entirely.
[00:10] It is a profound, radical commitment to postpone one's own final liberation until every single living being is free from suffering.
[00:20] At its heart, this vow is built on the twin pillars of wisdom and compassion.
[00:27] Wisdom allows us to see the fundamental interconnectedness of all life, while compassion is the natural result of that vision.
[00:35] The realization that my happiness is inseparable from yours.
[00:40] Taking this vow does not require you to be perfect today.
[00:44] Instead, it requires a shift in your fundamental direction.
[00:49] It means transforming every daily action, whether it is a difficult conversation at work or a simple moment of patience, into an act of service.
[00:59] You are essentially saying that you will become the best version of yourself, not for your own sake, but so you can be a more effective tool for the world.
[01:12] In a society that often prioritizes the individual, the Bodhisattva path offers a beautiful alternative: finding our greatest purpose in the well-being of others.
[01:22] By vowing to help all beings, we ironically find the very path that leads to our own enlightenment.
