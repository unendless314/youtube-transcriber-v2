# Why You Don't Owe Anyone an Explanation

[00:00] We are often taught that closure is a conversation, that we owe everyone an explanation for our boundaries, our no's, and our departures.
[00:08] We feel the urge to justify our peace, but here is a liberating truth.
[00:14] Explanation is a courtesy, not a debt.
[00:18] When you find yourself in a space where your values are compromised, where your energy is drained, or where your respect is non-negotiable, you don't need to hold a press conference.
[00:30] You don't need to stay and argue until the other person finally gets it.
[00:34] Some people are committed to misunderstanding you.
[00:38] In those moments, words aren't tools for clarity.
[00:42] They're just ammunition for further conflict.
[00:45] Walking away without explaining isn't cold.
[00:48] It's an act of self-stewardship.
[00:51] It's recognizing that your energy is too valuable to spend on a defense attorney for your own life.
[00:57] When you stop explaining, you stop giving others the power to negotiate your boundaries.
[01:01] Today, give yourself permission to just go.
[01:06] Let your absence be the only message they need.
[01:09] Choose your peace over their perspective.
