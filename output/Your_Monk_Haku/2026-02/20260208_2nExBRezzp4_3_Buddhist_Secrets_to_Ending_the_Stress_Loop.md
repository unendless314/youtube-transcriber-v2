# 3 Buddhist Secrets to Ending the Stress Loop

[00:00] Good morning everyone. If we look at Buddhist philosophy, joy isn't something we chase,
 it's something we uncover by clearing away the clutter of the mind.
 To live more joyfully, starting today, we can focus on three essential keys.
 The first is the power of presence or mindfulness. Most of our stress lives in a future that hasn't
 happened or a past we cannot change. Joy is found strictly in the here and now.
 When you are drinking tea, just drink tea. When you are walking, just walk.
 By being fully present, you stop missing the small miracles of your own life.
 The second key is the art of letting go, known as non-attachment.
 We often suffer because we try to grasp things that are naturally flowing away.
 Buddhist wisdom teaches us that this too shall pass. By practicing non-attachment,
 we don't love less. We simply stop demanding that people, status or moments stay the same forever.
 Letting go of that control creates the space for peace to enter. Finally, we find joy through radical
 compassion. True happiness is rarely a solo project. When we shift our focus from our own internal anxieties
 to the well-being of others, our heart expands. Compassion connects us to the world and reminds us
 that we are never truly alone. In short, be present, let go, and be kind. Thank you.
