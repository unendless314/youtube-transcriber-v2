# A One-Minute Guide to Morning Stillness #MorningMindset #InnerPeace #MorningRoutine

[00:00] We spend so much time decorating our external lives.
 But how much time do we spend furnishing our internal ones?
 A serene mindset isn't about the absence of noise.
 It's about finding the "mute" button within.
 To get there, we have to start with how we greet the day.
 Try implementing a five-minute buffer every morning.
 Before you dive into your inbox or scroll through social media,
 give your brain a few moments of intentional silence.
 Let your thoughts settle like silt in a glass of water
 until the view becomes clear.
 From that clarity, you can begin to audit your mental rent.
 We often let worries about the future or regrets from the past
 live in our heads rent-free.
 When a heavy thought arrives,
 ask yourself if it is serving you right now.
 If the answer is no, give it an eviction notice.
 Finally, embrace the "and" technique.
 You can feel the weight of a busy day
 and still be the person in control of your reactions.
 Acknowledging the chaos without letting it define you
 is the ultimate secret to composure.
 Remember, peace is a practice, not a destination.
 You don't need a mountaintop to find serenity.
 You just need to breathe and return to the present.
 Be kind to your mind today.
