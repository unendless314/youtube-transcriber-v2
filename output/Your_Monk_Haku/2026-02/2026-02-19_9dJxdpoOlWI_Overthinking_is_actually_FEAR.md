# Overthinking is actually FEAR

[00:00] Have you ever spent three hours deciding what to say in a 30-second email, or stayed awake at 2 a.m. replaying a conversation from three years ago?
[00:07] We call that careful consideration. We call it being thorough.
[00:13] But let's call it what it actually is: fear. Overthinking is just fear in a fancy suit.
[00:22] It's the ego's way of trying to predict a future we can't control or fix a past we can't change.
[00:30] We convince ourselves that if we just run the scenario one more time, we can somehow avoid the pain, the rejection, or the failure.
[00:38] But here's the truth: you cannot think your way into certainty.
[00:43] While you're busy analyzing every "what if," the "what is" is passing you by.
[00:48] Analysis. Paralysis doesn't solve problems. It just builds a cage out of questions.
[00:56] Confidence isn't the absence of doubt. It's the willingness to act despite it.
[01:01] The next time you find yourself spiraling, stop and ask: Am I solving a problem, or am I just afraid of the outcome?
[01:10] Don't let your mind become a waiting room for a life you're too scared to start.
[01:14] Trust your gut, make the choice, and remember: A wrong decision creates experience, but no decision creates nothing.
