# 5 Lessons From a Buddhist Monk on Becoming Your Own Refuge

[00:00] Human beings spend much of their lives searching for something to hold on to.
[00:03] We look for security in people, in wealth, in status, in plans carefully arranged to protect us from uncertainty.

[00:16] We gather possessions and call them stability.

[00:36] When expectations collapse, fear arises.

[01:00] Even in moments of pleasure, there is unease, because part of us already senses that what we hold will not remain.
[01:08] The Buddha offered a simple yet profound invitation.
[01:11] Become your own refuge.
[01:13] Not in isolation.
[01:14] Not in cold independence.
[01:16] But in deep inner stability that does not depend on what changes.
[01:21] To become your own refuge means to cultivate a mind that can remain steady in gain and loss, in praise and blame, in joy and sorrow.
[01:30] It means discovering a ground within that is not shaken by the movements of the world.

[03:43] It is not withdrawal from responsibility.
[03:46] It is not the construction of emotional walls.

[04:04] While emotions rise and fall, wisdom can observe without being overwhelmed.

[04:21] We no longer cling to passing states as if they define us.
