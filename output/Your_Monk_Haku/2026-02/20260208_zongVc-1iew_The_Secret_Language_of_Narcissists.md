# The Secret Language of Narcissists

[00:00] Have you ever been at the receiving end of a verbal attack that felt completely disconnected
 from reality? Maybe you were called a liar, a failure, or disgusting by someone,
 and it left you questioning your own character. Before you take those words to heart, consider this:
 The biggest misconception about narcissists is that they love themselves.
 In reality, narcissism is a disorder of self-loathing.
 Deep down, they carry a weight of ugly insecurities and misery that is too heavy for them to face.
 To survive, they use a psychological defense called "projection".
 When they scream that you are the problem, they are actually describing themselves.
 Their insults are not a window into your soul. They are a mirror reflecting their own internal battle.
 Understanding this changes everything. You don't have to defend yourself against a lie.
 When you realize that their anger is just a confession of their own pain,
 you stop being a target and start being a witness.
 The best revenge isn't a comeback. It's the peace of knowing their storm isn't about you.
