# The 1 Mistake Killing Your Progress

[00:00] Most of us are waiting for a moment that doesn't actually exist. We tell ourselves we'll start that project, leave that situation, or chase a goal once we feel ready. We're waiting for a surge of confidence or a perfect alignment of the stars to signal that it's time to move. But here is the truth that keeps us stuck. Action doesn't follow confidence. Confidence follows action.
[00:29] If you wait until the fear is gone to start, you'll be waiting forever.
[00:34] Fear isn't a stop sign. It's just a passenger. The mistake isn't that you're afraid or unsure.
[00:43] It's the belief that you need to stop feeling those things before you can begin.
[00:49] You don't need a five-year plan or a burst of inspiration. You just need to lower the bar for success until it's impossible to fail. Don't try to leap. Just shuffle your feet forward.
[00:59] Stop waiting for the right feeling. The feeling follows the doing.
[01:05] Do one small imperfect thing today and watch how the stuckness starts to melt away.
