# The Beauty of a Closed Chapter

[00:00] Please raise your glasses. Tonight, we aren't here to dwell on the goodbyes or the what-ifs.
 Instead, I'd like to propose a toast to the love we once had.
 It's easy to look back at a closed chapter and only see the ending, but that ignores the beauty
 of the pages written before it. We are, in many ways, a collection of the people who have loved us
 and the people we have tried to love well. So here's to the laughter that felt like it would
 never end, the lessons that were hard-won, and the person we became because of that connection.
 We honor that time not as a failure but as a vital part of our story, to the memories that stay warm,
 the growth that came from the cold, and the love that, even if it didn't last forever,
 was real while it was ours, to what was.
