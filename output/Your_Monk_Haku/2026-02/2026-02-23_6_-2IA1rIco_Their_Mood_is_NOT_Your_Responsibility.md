# Their Mood is NOT Your Responsibility

[00:00] We've all been there. Someone walks into the room trailing a cloud of gloom, and suddenly, you feel like it's your job to fix the weather. You start walking on eggshells or performing emotional CPR just to bring them back to baseline. But here is the liberating truth.
[00:18] You are a companion, not an emotional thermostat. Taking responsibility for someone else's mood doesn't actually help them. It just exhausts you. While it feels like kindness, it often creates a cycle where they never learn to self-regulate, and you lose your own peace of mind. There is a vital difference between empathy and ownership. You can offer a listening ear without becoming a sponge, and you can be compassionate toward their bad day without letting it become yours.
[00:52] Everyone has the right to feel their feelings, even the uncomfortable ones. When we try to fix someone's mood, we are usually just trying to soothe our own discomfort.
[01:03] Today, give yourself permission to stay centered. If someone is struggling, be kind and be present, but stay firmly in your own lane. Your only true responsibility is to manage your own reaction.
[01:20] Their mood is a reflection of their internal world, not a performance review of your worth.
