# The REAL Reason You Feel Stuck

[00:00] There is a reason you feel stuck, and it is not what you think.
[00:03] The mind has been taught to interpret stillness as failure.
[00:06] If you are not moving, you must be broken.
[00:09] If you do not have a plan, you must be behind.
[00:12] But this is not truth.
[00:14] This is conditioning.

[00:23] And that gap is not a sign that something is wrong with you.
[00:27] It is a natural passage, and like all natural things, it deserves not your resistance, but your attention.
[00:33] You do not need to have all the answers right now.
[00:36] You do not need to see the entire path.

[00:44] The path does not appear ahead of you.
