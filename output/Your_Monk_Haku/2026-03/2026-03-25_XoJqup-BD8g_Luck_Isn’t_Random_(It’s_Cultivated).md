# Luck Isn’t Random (It’s Cultivated)

[00:00] We often talk about luck as if it's a lightning strike, something that either hits you or it doesn't. But the truth is, luck is a byproduct of the environment you cultivate. Most of us understand the first step, you have to get your own house in order. You have to stabilize your mental health and find your own footing. However, where almost everyone fails is at the second step.
[00:20] We mistakenly believe that being a good person means anchoring ourselves to every unstable person in our orbit, choosing to struggle alongside them as a badge of honor. But loyalty to dysfunction isn't a virtue, it's a trap. To actually invite luck into your life, you must build relationships with people who have also done the work. You need a circle that operates on clarity and resilience rather than constant crisis management. You are not failing others by choosing health. You are finally giving yourself the permission to thrive. If you want better outcomes, you need better company. Stop romanticizing the shared struggle and start prioritizing the shared growth.
