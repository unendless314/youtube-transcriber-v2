# Buddhist Wisdom: The Bridge of Right Intention

[00:00] Life is a bridge suspended over turbulent waters.
[00:02] On one side is uncertainty, regret, and stagnation.
[00:06] On the other is clarity, purpose, and fulfillment.
[00:09] The only way to cross is by laying down planks, and each one of those planks is a conscious goal.
[00:14] Most people never build their bridge.
[00:17] They stand at the edge, lost in vague wishes, waiting for direction to appear.
[00:22] But direction isn't found. It's created.
[00:25] Buddhism teaches the principle of right intention.
[00:29] The clarity of purpose that eliminates suffering.
[00:31] Most pain doesn't come from failure.
[00:34] It comes from wandering without a map and chasing what isn't meant for you.
[00:38] Too many people set goals like fragile paper boats.
[00:41] Vague, weak, and easily sunk.
[00:44] They say they want to be successful or happy.
[00:47] But these are destinations without a path.
[00:49] To build a bridge that holds, you must be specific.
[00:52] Stop saying, I want to be healthier, and start affirming, I am a person who nourishes my body.
[00:59] Replace, I want to start a business with, I am a mindful entrepreneur creating with purpose.
[01:04] When you use, I am statements, you aren't just thinking about change.
[01:08] You are becoming the change.
[01:10] Is your bridge built on wishes or blueprints?
[01:13] Every day you hesitate, the water of doubt rises higher.
[01:17] The first plank is the hardest, but the moment you define your goal with clarity, you create momentum, focus on your path, build with intention, and step forward, one plank at a time.
