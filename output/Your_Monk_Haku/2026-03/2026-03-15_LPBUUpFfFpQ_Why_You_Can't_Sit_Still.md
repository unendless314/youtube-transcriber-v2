# Why You Can't Sit Still

[00:00] Most of us have never been taught to sit with discomfort.
[00:02] From a very early age, we are taught to solve, to fix, to move quickly from the problem to the answer, from the question to the resolution.
[00:11] And so when something cannot be solved, when the uncertainty is not a puzzle, but simply the condition of our lives at this moment, we do not know what to do with ourselves.
[00:19] We distract. We worry.
[00:22] We fill the silence with noise so we do not have to feel the openness beneath us.
[00:27] But there is another way.
[00:30] In the Zen tradition, there is a practice sometimes called "don't know mind".
[00:33] It is not ignorance.
[00:35] It is not giving up.
[00:37] It is the deliberate act of resting in a question without forcing it toward an answer.
[00:42] It is the recognition that the open space of not knowing is not a problem to be closed.
[00:48] It is a space in which something true can emerge, if we are willing to wait and to listen.
