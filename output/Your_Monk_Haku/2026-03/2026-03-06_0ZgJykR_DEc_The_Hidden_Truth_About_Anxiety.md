# The Hidden Truth About Anxiety

[00:00] We often talk about anxiety as if it's just worrying too much or having a busy mind, but anyone who has felt it knows the truth is much heavier.
[00:08] The truth of anxiety is that it's a physical experience as much as a mental one.
[00:13] It's the sudden racing of a heart for no reason, the shallow breath and the persistent knot in your stomach that refuses to untie.
[00:19] It is your body's alarm system stuck in the on position, screaming of a danger that isn't actually there.
[00:24] It's also incredibly isolating.
[00:27] Anxiety is the voice that tells you everyone is judging you or that the worst-case scenario isn't just possible, it's inevitable.
[00:34] It makes you feel like you're failing at things that look easy for everyone else.
[00:38] But here is the most important truth.
[00:40] Anxiety is not a character flaw.
[00:43] Having it doesn't mean you are weak or broken.
[00:46] It means your system is working overtime to protect you, even if it's misguided.
[00:51] You are not your anxiety.
[00:53] You are the person experiencing it, and that distinction is where your power lives.
[00:58] By acknowledging the struggle, we take away its secrecy.
[01:01] We learn that we aren't alone in the shadows, and that even when the alarm is loud, we are still safe.
