# Charlie Puth’s "House" is a Life Lesson

[00:00] We spend a massive amount of our energy building houses.
[00:03] We work for the job title, the GPA, the curated social media feed, and the material markers of success.
[00:11] We follow the blueprint that society gave us, assuming that if we just get the walls high enough and the paint polished enough, we'll finally feel secure.
[00:19] But Charlie Puth reminds us of a painful truth in his latest song, "House."
[00:23] You can own the structure and still feel empty inside of it.
[00:26] The lesson here is about intent versus appearance.
[00:30] A house is a shell, a home is the soul.
[00:34] You can build a house of a career, but if it lacks passion, it's just a cold building.
[00:40] You can build a house of a relationship based on how it looks to others, but without vulnerability, you're just roommates in a hollow space.
[00:47] The takeaway?
[00:49] Don't get so caught up in the construction of your life that you forget to inhabit it.
[00:53] Don't just build the walls, fill the rooms.
[00:56] Stop asking, "What does my life look like?" and start asking, "What does it feel like to live here?"
[01:02] Because at the end of the day, a roof doesn't mean much if you're shivering underneath it.
