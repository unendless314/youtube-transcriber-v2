# Fear is Not Your Enemy

[00:00] What if the thing you've been running from your entire life is actually trying to show you the way to love? Fear is not your enemy. In Buddhist teaching, fear is a messenger, and when you stop running from it, when you finally turn around and look at it honestly, it begins to reveal exactly where your heart is still closed. There is a practice that transforms fear, not by destroying it, but by sitting with it the way you would sit beside a troubled friend, with patience, with kindness, without the need to fix anything. And when that shift happens, when fear is no longer something to escape but something to understand, something in the heart quietly opens. This is the teaching of loving presence, and it begins with the very thing you are most afraid of. The full teaching is on this channel. Five reflections, approximately 30 minutes.
