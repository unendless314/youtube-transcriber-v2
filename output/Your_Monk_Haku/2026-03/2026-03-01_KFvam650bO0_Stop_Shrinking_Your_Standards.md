# Stop Shrinking Your Standards

[00:00] We've all been there. Someone lets us down, stays silent when they should speak, or treats us with less respect than we deserve. And what do we do? We become their defense attorney. We say, they're just stressed, or they didn't mean it that way, or they had a hard childhood.
[00:12] While empathy is a gift, using it to build a bridge for someone who isn't even trying to cross it is a trap. When you make excuses for people, you aren't being kind, you are being dishonest. You are choosing to fall in love with their potential rather than acknowledging their reality. The truth is simple. People prioritize what they care about. If they wanted to be there, they would. If they valued the boundary, they'd respect it. Stop shrinking your standards to fit their lack of effort. When you stop making excuses, you finally leave room for the people who don't need them. Choose the clarity of the truth over the comfort of a lie.
