# Are you leading or just busy?

[00:00] There is an old saying that has echoed through monastery walls for centuries: "You cannot pour water from an empty vessel." And yet, how many of us rush to guide others before we have found steady ground beneath our own feet? The impulse to help is beautiful, it is one of the most noble stirrings of the human heart. But impulse alone is not enough.
[00:20] A man who has not yet learned to sit quietly with his own suffering will only add confusion to the confusion of others. A woman who preaches patience but burns with anxiety in her private hours. She teaches more through her hidden life than through her spoken words. And what she teaches, without meaning to, is contradiction. The Buddha did not begin his ministry the morning after his awakening. He sat. He remained. He allowed the truth to settle into every fiber of his being before he opened his mouth and turned the wheel of Dharma. This was not selfishness. This was the deepest form of preparation. When we lead by living well, we do not need to announce ourselves. There is no need for grand proclamations or the performance of wisdom. People sense integrity the way they sense warmth from a fire. They do not need to be told the fire is hot. They simply feel it and they draw near.
