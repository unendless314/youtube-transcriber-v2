# The Real Reason You’re Anxious (It’s Not What You Think)

[00:00] we often blame our anxiety on the world around us we point to the uncertain times or the unknown future as the source of our stress but let's be honest with ourselves uncertainty isn't the enemy uncertainty is a constant it is the only thing we can actually count on to remain the same throughout our lives the truth is i'm not anxious because life is unpredictable i'm anxious because i never learned the tools to handle that unpredictability i've spent my energy trying to control the outcome instead of building the mental strength to survive whatever happens we treat peace like something we find in a stable environment when real peace is a skill we develop internally it's time to stop waiting for the world to become certain and start doing the work to become resilient the goal isn't to know what's coming it's to know that i'll be okay regardless of what comes
