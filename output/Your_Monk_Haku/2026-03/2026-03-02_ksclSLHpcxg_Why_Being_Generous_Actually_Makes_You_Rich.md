# Why Being Generous Actually Makes You Rich

[00:00] There is a common fear that the more we give, the less we have, as if our lives were a simple math equation. But the reality of generosity is that it follows the laws of growth. Generosity does not make you poor because wealth is not just the balance in a bank account. When you give, you are building trust and deepening relationships. True poverty is not an empty wallet. It is an empty spirit. By holding on to everything with a clenched fist, we keep ourselves from receiving anything new. But an open hand can both give and receive. Generosity expands your perspective, helping you realize that you already have enough to share. Ultimately, we are not remembered for what we kept, but for what we gave away. The more you pour into others, the fuller your own cup becomes.
