# The Difference Between Pain and Suffering

[00:00] I want to ask you something honest.
[00:01] How much of your suffering right now is caused by what is actually happening?
[00:05] And how much is caused by your demand that it be different?
[00:08] The Buddha called this dukkha.
[00:10] And it is not just grief or loss or illness.
[00:13] It is the quiet, persistent ache of a mind that is always arguing with reality.
[00:17] Pain is what life sometimes brings.
[00:19] Suffering is what the mind adds on top of it.
[00:22] A man loses his work.
[00:23] That is pain.
[00:24] Then he spends three years telling himself he is a failure, that his best days are behind him.
[00:29] That is suffering.
[00:30] And that suffering was built entirely from the habit of demanding life be other than it is.
[00:35] When you stop arguing with what is, you do not become passive.
[00:38] You become clear.
[00:39] And from clarity, you can finally move.
