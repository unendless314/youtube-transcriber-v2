# Small Habits, Massive Change

[00:00] There is a crack in a stone beside a river. It was not made by a hammer. It was not made by force.
[00:04] It was made by a single drop of water falling in the same place, every day, for longer than any of us has been alive. This is what your small habits are doing inside you right now. Even when you cannot feel it, even when you are convinced nothing is changing, the drop does not need to see the crack to keep falling. It only needs to fall. If you want to understand how the smallest actions can reshape an entire life, the full teaching is on this channel. Watch it. And then, Tomorrow morning, let one drop fall.
