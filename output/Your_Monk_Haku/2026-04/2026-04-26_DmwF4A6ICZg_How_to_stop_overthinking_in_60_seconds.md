# How to stop overthinking in 60 seconds

[00:00] I want to say something that I hope you will carry with you today.
[00:02] You are not your thoughts.
[00:04] You are the awareness that watches them.
[00:06] Think of the sky.
[00:07] Clouds pass through it.
[00:09] Dark clouds.
[00:10] Heavy clouds.
[00:11] Storms that feel like they will never end.
[00:13] But the sky does not become the storm.
[00:15] It remains open, unchanged.
[00:18] Your mind is the sky.
[00:19] Your thoughts are the clouds.
[00:21] Toxic thoughts feel permanent because we hold them so close.
[00:25] But they are not permanent.
[00:27] They are passing, like everything else.
[00:30] The moment you stop identifying with the thought and begin to simply observe it, something shifts.
[00:35] The thought loses its power, not because you destroyed it, but because you stopped believing it was you.
