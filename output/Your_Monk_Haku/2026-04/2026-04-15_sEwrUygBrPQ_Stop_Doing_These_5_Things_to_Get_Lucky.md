# Stop Doing These 5 Things to Get Lucky

[00:00] We often talk about luck as if it's a lightning bolt, something that strikes the chosen few out of thin air. But more often than not, luck isn't about what you're chasing, it's about what you've stopped doing. You see, luck has a hard time finding a life cluttered with chaos. You find luck when you don't let small problems compound until they become crises, and you don't destroy your health for temporary gains. You find it when you don't stay in environments that trade your peace for anxiety, and you don't waste breath on arguments that won't matter in the long run.
[00:30] Real fortune favors those who don't burn bridges over ego, don't twist themselves to fit into a sick society, and don't let the rust of cynicism eat their spirit. Above all, you find luck when you don't disrespect yourself by giving your time to people who simply do not care about you.
[00:46] When you clear away that noise, you create a vacuum. Suddenly, you have the time and the clarity to invest in yourself, your family, and the goals that truly matter. Luck isn't just a coincidence, it's the space you've cleared for opportunity to finally land. Stop doing the things that drain you, and watch how quickly the world starts working for you.
