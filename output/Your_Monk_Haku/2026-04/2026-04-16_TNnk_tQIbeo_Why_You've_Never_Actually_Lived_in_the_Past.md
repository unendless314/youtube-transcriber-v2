# Why You've Never Actually Lived in the Past

[00:00] you have never lived in the past. You have only ever remembered it from the present.
[00:04] You have never lived in the future. You have only ever imagined it from the present.
[00:09] Every moment of joy you have ever felt, every conversation that mattered, every instant of being truly alive, all of it happened here, in the now. And yet the mind moves constantly, backward into what was lost, forward into what might go wrong, and while it travels, this breath, this moment, this one irreplaceable instant of being alive, passes without being touched. There is a Zen story of a monk who was asked, "What is enlightenment?" He said, "When I am hungry, I eat. When I am tired, I sleep."
[00:43] The student said, "Everyone does that." And the monk replied, "No. When most people eat, they are thinking of a hundred other things. When they sleep, they are carrying tomorrow on their chest.
[00:55] Your life is not waiting for you somewhere else. It is here. It is now. It always has been."
