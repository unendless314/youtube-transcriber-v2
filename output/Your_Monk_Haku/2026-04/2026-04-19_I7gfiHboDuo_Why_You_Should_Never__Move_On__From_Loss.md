# Why You Should Never "Move On" From Loss

[00:00] People say after a great loss, "I am trying to move on."
[00:03] But moving on is not the goal. Moving forward is. And there is a difference.
[00:07] Moving on suggests leaving them behind. Moving forward means bringing them with you.
[00:12] You honor the ones you have lost by letting them live through how you love others.
[00:16] The grief and the gratitude can exist together.
[00:18] That is not weakness. That is a heart that has been broken open by love.
