# The Power of Doing Nothing (Wu Wei)

[00:00] Most people, when life becomes overwhelming, do the same thing: they do more. They push harder.
[00:06] They add another plan, another strategy, another layer of effort on top of an already exhausted life. And when that does not work, they push harder still. But consider water. Soft, yielding, formless water, and yet given enough time, it carves canyons through solid rock. Not by force, by patience, by moving in harmony with what is, rather than against it. The ancient teachers called this wu-wei, non-forcing. The art of acting in accordance with the nature of things, rather than imposing your will upon them. And it is one of the most misunderstood forms of power that exists. You do not always need to do more. Sometimes you need to do less. And trust that the river knows where it is going.
