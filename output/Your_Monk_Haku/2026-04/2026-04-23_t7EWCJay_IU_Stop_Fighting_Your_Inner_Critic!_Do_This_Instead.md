# Stop Fighting Your Inner Critic! Do This Instead

[00:00] Most people when they discover the inner critic want to fight it, they argue with it, they try to prove it wrong, they declare that they are done listening. And it never works. Because when you fight the critic you are still inside the battle. And the battle itself is the suffering. There is another way. Instead of meeting the voice with more harshness, you meet it with compassion.
[00:23] Not because it deserves it, but because you do. You turn toward it the way you would turn toward a frightened child. You say, "I see that you are afraid. I see that you have been trying to protect me, but I do not need this protection anymore." Every time you do this, even once, even imperfectly, something softens, something old begins to release. Self-acceptance does not come from winning an argument with yourself. It comes from learning to be kind to yourself in the moments when kindness feels the hardest.
