# Stop Believing the Voice in Your Head

[00:00] There is a voice in your head that criticizes you before you have done anything wrong that replays old conversations, that tells you that you are not enough, and for most of your life you have believed that voice is you. It is not. The Buddha taught something that changes everything once you truly understand it. Thoughts arise on their own. They appear, they linger, and they pass, like clouds crossing a sky. And you are not the clouds. You are the sky. The sky does not become a storm. It holds the storm. And the moment you learn to hold your thoughts instead of becoming them, that is the moment real freedom begins.
