# Nothing Lasts. That's the Good News.

[00:00] Try this once today. Stop. Completely stop. Name three things that are real and present in your life right now. Not what you are working toward. Not what you are afraid of losing. What is actually here? A sound. A warmth. A person. Let yourself feel that they are real. And that they are enough. This is not a small exercise. This is the entire Buddhist teaching on impermanence distilled into one minute. Nothing lasts. Which means what is here right now deserves your full attention. Not tomorrow. Now.
