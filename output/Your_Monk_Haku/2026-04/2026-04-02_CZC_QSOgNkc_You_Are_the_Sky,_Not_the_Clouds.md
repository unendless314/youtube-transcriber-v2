# You Are the Sky, Not the Clouds

[00:00] Look at the sky right now. When clouds cover it, is it less sky? When a storm moves through, does it become smaller? The sky does not change. It simply holds whatever passes through it. The storms come, the clear days come, and the sky remains exactly what it always was. You are like that sky. Every rejection you have ever experienced, every time a door closed, every time a hand did not reach for you, that was weather. It passed through you, but it was never you. The employer who did not hire you saw a document, an hour of your time, a snapshot. They did not see your depth, they did not see what you carry. They made a decision with incomplete information, the way every human being does, and you took that decision and carved it into your identity. Rejection tells you about circumstances, about timing, about the limitations of another person's vision. It has never, not once, told you what you are worth. Watch the full teaching on the channel today. Let it sit
[00:52] with you. Let it reach the places that rejection has been living. You were never the clouds. You were always the sky.
