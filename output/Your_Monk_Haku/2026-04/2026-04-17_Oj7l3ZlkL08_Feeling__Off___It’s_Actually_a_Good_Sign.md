# Feeling "Off"? It’s Actually a Good Sign

[00:00] You woke up one day and something felt different, not broken, not wrong, just different.
[00:07] Like the life you have been living no longer fits the way it used to.
[00:12] Most people, when they feel this way, assume something is wrong.
[00:16] They search for the source.
[00:18] They look for the mistake they must have made.
[00:21] But what if nothing is wrong?
[00:23] What if this discomfort is not the sign of something breaking, but the sensation of something opening?
[00:30] In Zen, before a student reaches a new level of awareness, they almost always pass through a period of great restlessness.
[00:36] The teachers do not panic.
[00:38] They do not rush to return the student to calm.
[00:41] They recognize it for what it is, the growing edge of a life expanding beyond its previous boundaries.
[00:47] You have felt this before.
[00:49] Before every significant change in your life, there was first this feeling.
[00:54] It is here again, and it is not here to harm you.
[00:58] It is here to tell you, something in you is already becoming new.
