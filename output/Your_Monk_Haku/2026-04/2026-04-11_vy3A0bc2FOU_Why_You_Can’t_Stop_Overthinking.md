# Why You Can’t Stop Overthinking

[00:00] Most of your suffering is not caused by what happened to you.
[00:02] It is caused by what you did after.
[00:05] The Buddha spoke of two arrows.
[00:07] The first arrow is the painful event, the harsh word, the disappointment, the loss.
[00:13] That arrow is real, and you did not choose it.
[00:17] But then most people reach for a second arrow and drive it into the same wound themselves, through blame, through rumination, through replaying the moment for hours, days, sometimes years.
[00:29] The first arrow is life.
[00:30] The second arrow is always your own hand.
[00:33] The unbothered mind does not pretend the first arrow did not land.
[00:37] It simply learns to put down the second one.
[00:40] If you want to know how, the full teaching is on the channel.
[00:44] Go find it.
[00:45] It may be the most useful thing you watch this week.
