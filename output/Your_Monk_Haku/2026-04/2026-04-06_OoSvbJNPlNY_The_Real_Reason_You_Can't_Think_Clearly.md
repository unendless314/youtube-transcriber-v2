# The Real Reason You Can't Think Clearly

[00:00] There is a teaching about a pond. When the water is constantly disturbed, you cannot see the bottom.
[00:05] Everything is murky. You cannot find clarity, cannot find direction, cannot find peace.
[00:12] But when the water is allowed to grow still, the mud settles. And suddenly you can see all the way to the riverbed. Your mind is this pond. Every notification, every reaction, every compulsive reach for your phone. That is the wind disturbing the water. And you wonder why you cannot think clearly. You wonder why the answers never come. They cannot come through the noise. They never could. Stillness is not emptiness. Stillness is where everything becomes visible.
