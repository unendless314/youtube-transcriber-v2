# When You Feel Lost, Watch This.

[00:00] There is a moment in every crossing when you can no longer see the shore you left, and you cannot yet see the shore ahead. A person standing in that moment might easily believe they are lost, but they are not. They are simply between this space, between who you were and who you are becoming, is disorienting. Nothing feels solid. The mind demands resolution. It says, choose, decide, define yourself. But the open question is not an emergency, it is an invitation. Trust the feeling of difference. The life that is coming will arrive the way morning arrives. Not because you commanded it, but because the darkness, having done its work, simply gave way to light. You are not lost, you are between, and between is the most alive place you have ever stood.
