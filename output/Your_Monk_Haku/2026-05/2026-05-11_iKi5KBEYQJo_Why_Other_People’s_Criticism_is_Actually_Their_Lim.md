# Why Other People’s Criticism is Actually Their Limit

[00:00] We spend an incredible amount of energy auditioning for people who aren't even watching the show.
[00:04] We trim our ambitions and quiet our voices to avoid the judgment of those who never had our best interests in mind. But here is the truth. Most people are navigating their own hidden agendas and private insecurities. When you realize that their criticism is usually just a reflection of their own limits, you stop carrying the weight of their expectations. The moment you stop looking left and right for approval, you start moving forward with speed. People will call this luck.
[00:31] They'll say you were in the right place at the right time or that things just seem to fall into your lap. In reality, it isn't luck. It's the result of a clear vision that is no longer being blurred by the opinions of others. When you focus entirely on your own path, you see opportunities that were always there. You were just too busy worrying to notice them. Stop living for the spectators and start living for the vision.
