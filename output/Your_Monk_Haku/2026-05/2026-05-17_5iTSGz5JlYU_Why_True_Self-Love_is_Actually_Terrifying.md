# Why True Self-Love is Actually Terrifying

[00:00] We often talk about the self-love journey as if it's all bubble baths and affirmations.
[00:04] But let's be honest, true self-love is incredibly tough.
[00:08] It requires facing the one person we spend our whole lives trying to distract ourselves from.
[00:13] Ourselves.
[00:15] Think about how many people can't bear the thought of watching a movie in a crowded theater alone.
[00:20] Or how many would love to see a beautiful destination but won't take that solo road trip because the silence in the car feels too loud.
[00:27] We've become terrified of our own company.
[00:30] But leaning into that discomfort is exactly what opens the door to a better life.
[00:34] When you finally have the courage to take that trip, to sit in that theater, or to simply exist with your own thoughts, something shifts.
[00:42] You stop waiting for someone else to validate your existence or show you the world.
[00:47] The journey isn't easy, but learning to love your own company is the ultimate freedom.
[00:52] Because once you are truly at peace with yourself, you can go anywhere.
