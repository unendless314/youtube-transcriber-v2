# The Danger of Being a Perfect Parent

[00:00] As parents, our deepest instinct is to protect our children.
[00:02] We want to clear the rocks from their path, shield them from heartbreak, and save them from the exact same blunders we made when we were younger.
[00:09] But here is a hard truth we all need to face.
[00:12] When you refuse to let your kids make the mistakes you needed to make to grow, you aren't protecting them, you are depriving them.
[00:18] Think back to your own greatest life lessons.
[00:21] Did they come from a parent giving you a perfect, obstacle-free path?
[00:25] No.
[00:26] They came from the scraped knees, the failed projects, the broken hearts, and the poor choices.
[00:31] We learned resilience because we had to bounce back.
[00:35] We learned wisdom because we tasted the consequences of foolishness.
[00:39] When we helicopter over our children and smooth out every wrinkle, we steal their opportunity to build grit.
[00:45] We send them the silent, damaging message, I don't think you are strong enough to handle this.
[00:50] Our job isn't to raise flawless children, it's to raise capable, resilient adults.
[00:56] So, let them fail.
[00:57] Let them make the mess.
[00:58] Be the safety net that catches them, not the cage that keeps them from flying.
