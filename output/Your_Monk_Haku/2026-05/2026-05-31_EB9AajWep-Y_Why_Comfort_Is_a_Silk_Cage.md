# Why Comfort Is a Silk Cage

[00:00] You think comfort is freedom, but comfort, when it becomes the only goal, becomes a cage made of silk.
[00:05] You do not feel the bars. That is precisely what makes it dangerous.
[00:09] The ancient teachers did not warn against pain. Pain was honest. Pain pointed somewhere.
[00:16] What they warned against was the slow numbing that comes when a life is arranged entirely around avoiding discomfort.
[00:22] When you spend your years optimizing for ease, you do not arrive at peace.
[00:26] You arrive at emptiness, with good furniture.
[00:29] Comfort is not the enemy, but comfort as a destination is a road that leads nowhere.
