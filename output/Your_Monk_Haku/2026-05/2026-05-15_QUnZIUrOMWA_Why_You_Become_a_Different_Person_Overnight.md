# Why You Become a Different Person Overnight

[00:00] There is an identity you have built over decades, layer by layer, through experience, habit, and choice. By now you believe you know who you are, the kind of person who is not patient, who needs space, who struggles to be present. You wear these descriptions as though they were not chosen, but simply given. Then, in a single night, the child arrives, and by morning, you are someone else. The self you are so certain of becomes negotiable, then unimportant, then simply gone, replaced by something quieter and more honest. The teaching of no fixed self is almost impossible to understand intellectually, but hold a child who needs you absolutely, and it becomes a direct experience. You are never finished. The person you have decided you are is a working draft.
