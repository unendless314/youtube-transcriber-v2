# Why You Aren’t Happy Yet: The Reality vs Expectation Gap

[00:00] Most people have placed a condition on their own happiness without realizing it.
[00:04] They have quietly decided, I will be at peace when this changes, when that person apologizes, when I finally achieve enough, when life stops being so uncertain, and life with great patience continues to be exactly what it is. The Buddha taught that suffering is not caused by what happens to us, it is caused by the distance between what is happening and what we decided should be happening. That gap between reality and expectation is where all our anxiety lives, letting go is not weakness. It is not giving up on the people you love or the dreams that matter.
[00:33] It is releasing the condition, choosing to be present in the life you are actually living, instead of waiting for the one you planned.
