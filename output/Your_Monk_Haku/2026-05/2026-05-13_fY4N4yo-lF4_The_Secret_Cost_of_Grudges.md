# The Secret Cost of Grudges

[00:00] The person who hurt you has almost certainly moved on.
[00:02] They have built new days around them.
[00:05] They are living their life largely unbothered, while you have remained in the room where the wound first happened, tending it, revisiting it, keeping it alive.
[00:14] Holding a grudge is not strength.
[00:16] It is setting up a residence in the most painful room of the house and refusing to leave because leaving feels like letting them win.
[00:23] Forgiveness, as Buddhist teaching understands it, is not something you do for the person who harmed you.
[00:30] It is an act of self-liberation.
[00:31] It is the moment you decide your remaining peace is worth more than your remaining grievance.
[00:36] The years left to you are finite.
[00:39] Every day in that painful room is a day not spent in the garden, at the table, with the people who are still here and still love you.
