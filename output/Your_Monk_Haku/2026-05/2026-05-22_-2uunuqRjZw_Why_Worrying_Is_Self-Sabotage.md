# Why Worrying Is Self-Sabotage

[00:00] Most people think worry is protecting them. It is not. In the Buddhist tradition, we speak of the second arrow. When something painful happens in life, that is the first arrow. And it is real.
[00:12] But worry is the second arrow. The one you fire into yourself, you are wounded twice.
[00:18] Once by life. And once by your own thinking. The first arrow you could not always avoid.
[00:26] But the second one, that one is a choice. Today, put down the second arrow.
