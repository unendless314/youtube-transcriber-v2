# You Are NOT Late in Life. Watch This.

[00:00] Look at you, you made it. And yet somehow, right now, some of you are already convinced you are behind. Someone in your row has a job offer. Someone else is going to graduate school.
[00:09] Someone is moving to New York and starting a company and doing something that sounds impossibly impressive at parties. And you are sitting there thinking, what about me? This is one of the oldest forms of suffering the human mind has ever invented.
[00:21] Comparison. The belief that there is a correct timeline and everyone else is on it, and you somehow missed the orientation meeting. Buddhism teaches us something very simple about this. The present moment is the only place where your life is actually happening. Not when you get the job. Not when things fall into place. Here. Now. In the robe that does not quite fit. You are not late. You are not late. You are not lost. You are not lost. You are a human being at the beginning of something real. And that is not a problem. That is a gift.
