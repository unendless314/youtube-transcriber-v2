# How to Finally Uncover Your Inner Happiness!

[00:00] Everyone is trying to build happiness, more achievements, more approval, more certainty, more control. And yet the people who seem most at peace are not the ones who have the most, they are the ones who are holding the least. Happiness is not constructed, it is uncovered, it was always there. Beneath the expectations, beneath the resentments, beneath the long list of conditions you decided must be met before you allow yourself to rest, there are four things most worth releasing, the opinions of others, which are weather, not ground, the story of your past, which may be true but is no longer useful, the need to be right, which costs far more than it gives, and the fear that you are not enough, which was never true to begin with. Release these, slowly, without judgment, and watch how much room opens up inside you.
