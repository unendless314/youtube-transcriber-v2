# Stop planning your life. Do this instead.

[00:00] There is an old story about a monk who has asked for the secret to a good life.
[00:03] He poured his student a cup of tea. He poured it slowly, until the cup was full.
[00:07] And then he kept pouring. The tea spilled over the rim, onto the table, onto the floor.
[00:12] The student jumped back and said, Master, the cup is full. No more will go in.
[00:17] The monk sat down the teapot and said simply, Like this cup, you arrive here full.
[00:21] Full of expectations. Full of plans.
[00:23] Full of everything you have been told a good life should look like.
[00:26] Before I can offer you anything, you must first be willing to empty the cup.
[00:30] Class of 2026, you have worked for years to fill your cup.
[00:34] Today, I am asking you to empty it, because the life that waits for you is larger than anything you have been prepared to expect.
[00:41] More surprising, more difficult, and more beautiful.
