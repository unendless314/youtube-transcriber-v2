# The Best Cure for Anxiety? (No Free Time)

[00:00] We often spend our youth searching for purpose, as if it's a destination we haven't reached yet.
[00:04] We fill that search with a constant buzzing anxiety, worrying about the five-year plan, the perfect career, or our place in the world. Then kids arrive. Suddenly, your free time doesn't just shrink, it vanishes. You are no longer the protagonist of a philosophical drama.
[00:20] You are a snack-getter, a laundry folder, and a human pillow. But a strange thing happens in that chaos. You realize that a massive portion of your old anxiety wasn't deep existential dread.
[00:31] It was simply the byproduct of having too much time to think about yourself.
[00:35] When you have exactly four minutes to drink a coffee before a toddler scale shifts the living room, you don't have the luxury of wondering if you're living your best life. You're too busy actually living it. Kids strip away the noise. They teach you that purpose isn't found in a quiet room of reflection. It's found in the active, exhausted service of something other than your own ego.
[00:55] As it turns out, the void we were trying to fill wasn't empty. It was just waiting to be occupied.
