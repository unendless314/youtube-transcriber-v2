# This 1-Minute Shift Will Change Your Life

[00:00] You do not need to dismantle your life.
[00:01] You do not need to reject comfort or punish yourself for having enjoyed it.
[00:05] The problem was never the soft bed or the warm meal or the beautiful convenience of modern living.
[00:11] The problem is only when we let these things become the answer to a question they were never designed to address.
[00:17] So return, not dramatically, but steadily.
[00:21] Return to the people in front of you.
[00:23] Return to the work that asks something real of you.
[00:26] Return to the silence that frightens you a little.
[00:30] Because anything that frightens you a little is usually pointing towards something true.
[00:33] Bring yourself.
[00:35] Fully.
[00:36] That is where meaning lives.
[00:38] It has always lived there, waiting for you to arrive.
