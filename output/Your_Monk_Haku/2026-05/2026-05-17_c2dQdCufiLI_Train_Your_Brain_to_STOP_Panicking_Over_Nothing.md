# Train Your Brain to STOP Panicking Over Nothing

[00:00] Your brain does not know what year it is, it cannot tell the difference between a tiger in the grass and a difficult email in your inbox. Both trigger the same alarm, your chest tightens, your thoughts scatter, and you think something must be terribly wrong.
[00:12] But nothing is terribly wrong. Something is simply hard.
[00:17] The Buddha taught that suffering does not come only from what happens to us.
[00:22] It comes from how the mind meets what happens, and the mind can be trained.
[00:28] If you want to understand why your brain panics the moment things get difficult, and what to do about it, the full teaching is on the channel.
