# Are your thoughts weakening your aura?

[00:00] Spiritual interference is when your energy system gets bombarded with dense negative energy.
 And when that happens, many spiritual people revert to practices like energy clearing with
 sacred smoke to get rid of the interference. But here's what people often miss about spiritual
 interference. It doesn't matter how many times a day you clear your energy, if your mindset is
 disempowering, the sacred smoke won't do much. Your beliefs and the thoughts you habitually think
 heavily contribute to the overall vibration of your energy field. The more you allow your mind to get
 into negative loops, the more your vibration goes down, weakening your system and allowing for
 spiritual interference to affect you. So practice shifting to an optimistic mindset, knowing that
 the universe is always working in your favor and that it loves you unconditionally. Try these
 affirmations to help you out. There is no energy stronger than me. Everything is always working out
 for me. Out of this situation, only good will come.
