# Are You Telling The Same Story? Try This ✨❤️

[00:00] Whenever we are repetitively or obsessively telling a story to ourself or to others, so either you're stuck in your own mind in loads of mind loops or that's the story you keep telling others about what happened to you or what's happening to you, ask yourself, am I feeling this enough? Because stories usually don't carry a great deal of feeling. They're usually our mind's way and our way of trying to get in touch with something inside us that we're just not feeling and that we're just not popping open. So at the moment, if you are finding yourself consumed by certain stories in your life, in the world, just put your hand on your heart and ask yourself, what do I need to feel about this? What do I need to feel about this story that I keep telling my friend or my partner or my teacher or myself, this thing that keeps going round and round my head all week? What is it I really need to feel that's underneath the story?
