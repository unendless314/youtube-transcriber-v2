# Eckhart Tolle on the Moment He Woke Up

[00:00] I cannot live with myself any longer because I was so unhappy.
[00:04] I realized that the unhappiness was produced not by the circumstances of my life, but why couldn't I live with myself any longer?
[00:13] Because of my mind, how my mind interpreted all the things in my life.
[00:18] The story I was telling myself made me unhappy.
[00:22] I didn't know that.
[00:23] I didn't understand that fully.
[00:25] But another thought came out of that, and that thought said, if I can't live with myself, who am I and who is that self?
[00:34] Now, I didn't get an answer to that question on a conceptual level, but I got an answer experientially because at that moment, a separation occurred between the thought and the consciousness that had incarnated into the thought.
[00:49] And suddenly, I realized I is the consciousness.
