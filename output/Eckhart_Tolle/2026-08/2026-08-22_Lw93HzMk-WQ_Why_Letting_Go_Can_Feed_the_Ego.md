# Why Letting Go Can Feed the Ego

[00:00] I've spent time in Buddhist monasteries, I have also visited Christian monasteries, and I can tell you often, the good intentions don't actually work. They still have big egos, in many cases, with some exceptions. Self-image comes back as me, the one who has let go of everything and is therefore superior to you, who have not let go of everything. So the ego is so cunning and subtle. You have to be truly vigilant. So for most of you, you just have to deal with what life gives you or what life takes away from you. That's your practice.
