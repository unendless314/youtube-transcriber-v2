# The Ego Is Afraid. You Are Not.

[00:00] The most important thing in a person's life is their failures, because you're more important, I would say, than the successes. Your main learnings and your main transformations you experience through making mistakes and failing. Don't be afraid of failure. Ultimately, there is no failure. It's an essential part of your learning process. It's only the ego that is afraid of failure, because it has a certain image of who it is.
