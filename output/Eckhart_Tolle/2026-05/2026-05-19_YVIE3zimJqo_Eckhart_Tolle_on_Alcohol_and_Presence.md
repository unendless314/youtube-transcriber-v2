# Eckhart Tolle on Alcohol and Presence

[00:00] Refrain as much as possible from intoxicating beverages.
[00:04] However, if you cannot enjoy dinner without a glass of wine, then you can have a glass of wine.
[00:12] One glass of wine would not unduly interfere with the arising presence in you, but two might, and three will.
[00:21] So as little as possible, I recommend, none at all would be great.
[00:28] But since everybody here is spiritually advanced, if you do have a glass of wine, you will not be judged.
