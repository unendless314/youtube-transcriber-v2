# You Are Not Who You Think You Are

[00:00] The "I" that exists on the surface of life and the surface of life is your physical existence, your physical entity, all the so-called external things that surround you which ultimately are all mental phenomena. This is your surface reality, your surface "I". But your surface "I" is not just the physical realm. Your surface "I" is also the psychological entity of the ego in you, the invisible person with its conditioned mental emotional conditioning that also is the surface "I".
