# How the Buddha Explained Suffering | Eckhart Tolle

[00:00] The Buddha has summarized his teaching in one sentence, which is, I teach suffering and the end of suffering.
 Which means, I show you how suffering arises through your unconsciousness, and by realizing that, I show you how to transcend suffering.
