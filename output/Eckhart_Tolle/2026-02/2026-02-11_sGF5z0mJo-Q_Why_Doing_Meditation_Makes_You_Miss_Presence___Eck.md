# Why Doing Meditation Makes You Miss Presence | Eckhart Tolle

[00:00] Meditation, if it works as it should,
 ultimately the purpose is, the aim is to be present while you meditate,
 not to seek some future condition.
 This is a fundamental mistake that meditators make,
 is they imagine a future condition that they want to arrive at or achieve.
 And they think, if I just practice more and harder,
 I will eventually achieve that awakened state.
 And that belief is actually a hindrance.
 So, because then you are doing a meditation.
 And if you're doing a meditation,
 then you miss the one thing that is the awakened state.
 And we could call that being, the realization of being.
