# The Deepening Effect of Impermanence

[00:00] It's very important to sometimes be aware of your mortality, otherwise you live in some illusory realm and you see how temporary the physical form is. The contemplation of impermanence can have a deepening effect on you. In other words, the contemplation of death can have that deepening effect. To the ego, the egoic mind, it's very fearful. I'm the body and I'm going to die. How is that possible? How could the world exist without me? Mark Twain said, I have no fear of death. Before I was born, I had already been dead for millions of years and haven't suffered the slightest inconvenience from it. Well, that's true. Was it so horrible before you were born? I don't, I wasn't, I wasn't unhappy.
