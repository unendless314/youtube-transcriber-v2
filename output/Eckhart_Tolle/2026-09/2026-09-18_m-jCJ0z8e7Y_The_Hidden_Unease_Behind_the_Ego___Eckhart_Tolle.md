# The Hidden Unease Behind the Ego | Eckhart Tolle

[00:00] If you say I don't like myself, the way I look, or what I behave, or what I do, or whatever, there's a split that has happened, which is like a dysfunction in your mind, and that's the ego arises. It's as the Buddhists just call it the self, a sense of separate self, that is an image in your mind, and it's never at ease for very long. There's always a sense of unease in the background, and many humans don't even know that, because the sense of unease is always there, and if something is always there, you don't know it's there. It's like you have a humming noise at home, and let's say your fridge is making a noise, and it goes on for an hour, and suddenly it stops, and suddenly, wow, this humming noise was there for a long time. I didn't really know it anymore, so people cannot recognize their own states easily, especially if the state has been with you for very long.
