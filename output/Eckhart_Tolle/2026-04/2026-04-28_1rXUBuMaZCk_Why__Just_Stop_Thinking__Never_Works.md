# Why "Just Stop Thinking" Never Works

[00:00] There is awareness in you. You are not completely identified with the stream of thinking.
[00:07] But even though you are not completely identified with the stream of thinking, it may still be difficult even for you or some of you to simply say, I'm not going to think now for a minute.
[00:30] Is that a possibility? Don't hold your breath. It doesn't work.
