# What It Means to Be Spiritually Unconscious

[00:00] Unconscious in a spiritual sense means completely identified with the compulsive, incessant thought processes in your heads, identified with every thought that comes into your head. So when you identify with the thought, the thought has you in its grip to such an extent that every thought has a sense of self in it. So you derive your sense of self of who you are from the voice in the head. And that is for still millions of humans on the planet, that is still their daily reality.
