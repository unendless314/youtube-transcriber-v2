# Why Your Mind Keeps You Trapped in Darkness

[00:00] What kind of thoughts are you thinking while you are trapped in the dark night of the soul? What is your mind saying? It will be saying things but what it will be saying is it reflects the emotions that you feel and what your mind is thinking reflects the meaninglessness and perpetuates the sense of meaninglessness. So it says it's all so pointless. What's the point of it all? I've done all that work, it's come to nothing. Every thought that you have will be reinforcing what you feel. After a little while the negative mental emotional state can become part of your self, your sense of self. A delusion but it can become a very strong delusion and you are then trapped in an angry, deeply unhappy, unhappy sense of self. Some humans are trapped in that for years. So if you want
[01:05] to get out of that prison that you are trapped in, ask yourself, can I be aware of what my mind is saying? And no matter what your mind is saying, you are aware of it but you don't believe in it. Just let your mind say what it wants to say, just be aware of what what it is saying. This is the beginning of possibly becoming free of how your mind perpetuates and continuously recreates the dark night of the soul.
