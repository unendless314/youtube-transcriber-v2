# Why "I Think" Is an Illusion

[00:00] Most people don't think, because if you say I think, that implies that there is a volition behind it, that you have a choice in the matter, but you need some awareness to have a choice.
[00:11] And there's still millions of human on the planet.
[00:14] They do not have the sufficient awareness to know what's going on in their minds.
