# Why You Feel Like You Are Going Backward

[00:00] The evolution of consciousness for humanity is not a straight line that goes up.
[00:06] It goes in cycles. In the same way that the evolution of the planet goes in cycles, the evolution of consciousness goes in cycles. There's a cycle of growth, and a gain in consciousness, and then there's a cycle of regression, a loss of consciousness. And usually the cycle of regression is also associated with an increase in human suffering.
