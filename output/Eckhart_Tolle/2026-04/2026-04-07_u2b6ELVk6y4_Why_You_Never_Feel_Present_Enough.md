# Why You Never Feel Present Enough

[00:00] The present moment is rarely acknowledged in its fullness, and usually it's at best a stepping stone to the next moment, which, unconsciously again, not because you're telling yourself that, the next moment is somehow, or the next or the one after that, is somehow regarded as more important, potentially more fulfilling, than this moment.
[00:32] That's the mind pattern that we inherit from the surrounding culture, and that's a strange way to live if you consider that the present moment is all there ever is.
