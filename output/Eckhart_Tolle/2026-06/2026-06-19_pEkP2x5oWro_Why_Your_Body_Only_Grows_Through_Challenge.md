# Why Your Body Only Grows Through Challenge

[00:00] If you want to strengthen your body, you have to make life difficult for your body, for a while.
[00:07] For example, you're lifting weights.
[00:11] I can't do it, put it down. No, the body doesn't want to do it, but you're creating a huge challenge for the body.
[00:25] As the body meets the challenge, there is a demand for more energy, because there is a need for more energy.
[00:34] If there's no need, there's no demand, and the energy doesn't come.
[00:39] And after a while, the energy comes, the body gets stronger, and then you actually begin to enjoy the physical activity.
