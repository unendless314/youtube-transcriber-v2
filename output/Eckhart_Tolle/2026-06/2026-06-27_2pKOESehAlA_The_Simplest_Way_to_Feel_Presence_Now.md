# The Simplest Way to Feel Presence Now

[00:00] First thing you experience, if you are present, is an intensification of sensory perception. You become more alert to what's around you. Nature is particularly helpful. You look at a tree, the flower, the sky, the clouds, or you might see an animal, a bird. You follow the flight of the bird with your attention. And this is the primordial spiritual practice is sensory perception, which might involve listening to, and sometimes touching or smelling, but tasting even. You can drink a glass of water and really taste it without mental commentary.
