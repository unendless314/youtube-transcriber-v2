# The Buddha's Two Truths About Suffering

[00:00] The cornerstone of the Buddha's teaching, one of them is, there are basically two, one is no matter where you go and what you do, you're not going to be happy for long and you're going to find some degree or some measure of unhappiness in whatever form.
[00:17] The second truth of the Buddha is when he said in the scripture, I teach suffering and the end of suffering. When he said I teach suffering, he means I show you how you make yourself suffer and I teach the end of suffering means I show you how this unconscious pattern can come to an end.
