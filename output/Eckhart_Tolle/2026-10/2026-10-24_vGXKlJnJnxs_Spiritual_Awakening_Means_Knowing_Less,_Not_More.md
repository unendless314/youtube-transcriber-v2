# Spiritual Awakening Means Knowing Less, Not More

[00:00] In spiritual awakening, especially the early stages of spiritual awakening, it does not mean that you know more about spirituality, about yourself, about the universe.
[00:13] That can be misleading if you think that it will imply that you suddenly know many, many things about yourself, the universe, life.
[00:22] That's misleading.
[00:23] In fact, the truth is, the spiritual awakening means that you know less and less.
[00:31] And you become comfortable with knowing less and less, until you come to the place of not knowing.
[00:39] But this state of not knowing, you have risen above thinking.
[00:43] This state of not knowing is transcending the conceptual mind.
