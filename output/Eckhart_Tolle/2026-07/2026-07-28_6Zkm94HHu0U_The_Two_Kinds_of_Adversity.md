# The Two Kinds of Adversity

[00:00] Adversity comes in two forms.
[00:03] Adversity comes to you personally.
[00:06] It means a challenge that you face as an individual or in your family.
[00:12] And then there are other types of challenges that are collective.
[00:16] That means difficult situations that arise and affect large groups of people, sometimes millions of people or everybody on the planet at the same time.
[00:28] So when you talk about climate change, then that is certainly a collective challenge for humanity.
[00:34] And question arises, is there anything we can do about it?
[00:38] But more importantly, what is the underlying state of consciousness that is necessary for whatever action you take to be intelligent action, wise action, not action that instead of solving anything, makes it even worse.
