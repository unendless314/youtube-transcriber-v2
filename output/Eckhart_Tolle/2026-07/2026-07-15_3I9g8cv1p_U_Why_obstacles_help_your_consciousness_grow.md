# Why obstacles help your consciousness grow

[00:00] The very thing that seems to block the evolution of your consciousness is that which empowers the evolution of consciousness. It applies to all life forms. Often when a gardener puts his seeds in the soil, and quite often gardeners put more soil on top of the sprouting seed in order to strengthen the plant. The plant is strengthened because it encounters an obstacle. A plant that grows in a greenhouse, where it's totally protected, will be quite weak, will not develop the strength because it's not needed. The hindrance, the obstacle, is necessary for consciousness to grow. Consciousness needs to come up against something.
