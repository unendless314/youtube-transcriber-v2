# Why Eckhart Tolle Says God Does Not Exist

[00:00] God does not exist, because exist means to, ex means out, to stand out.
[00:07] God is not an entity in space and time.
[00:11] God cannot be known or understood.
[00:15] It would be absurd to try to know or understand, but I'm trying anyway.
[00:21] But this unnameable source of all life emanates into the light.
[00:30] Of God is consciousness, it emanates into this world of space and time where, gradually, God is awakening into this world and continuously incarnating into this world.
[00:44] And here, gradually, the universe is undergoing a gradual awakening process.
