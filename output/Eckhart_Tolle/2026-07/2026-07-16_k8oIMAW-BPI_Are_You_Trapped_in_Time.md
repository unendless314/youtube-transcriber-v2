# Are You Trapped in Time?

[00:00] The personal sense of self exists in the dimension of past and future. That's where time happens.
[00:10] And we have what looks like past and future, but can never be experienced directly because they don't exist in actuality. But we need them to move in this dimension. We need constant reference to past and future. But the question is, is it consuming us? Are we lost in it? Or are we able to use time for practical purposes, but not be trapped in it?
