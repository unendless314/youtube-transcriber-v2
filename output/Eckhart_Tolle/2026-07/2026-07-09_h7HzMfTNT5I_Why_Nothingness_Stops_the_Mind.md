# Why Nothingness Stops the Mind

[00:00] When you focus attention on nothing, the nothingness, the no-thingness of space, your mind becomes totally still. Otherwise you can't focus attention on it. So the same no-thingness that you see out there as outer space arises within you and is recognized as inner space. And that is the space of consciousness. Consciousness is formless and timeless.
[00:30] So by focusing attention on that which doesn't really exist, you can become aware of that in you that is the inner equivalent of space.
