# God's Pronoun Is I

[00:00] I had an imaginary conversation with God the other day, and I said, as you may know, humans have become very much concerned about their pronouns.
[00:12] We have been using the pronoun he to talk of you. Do you agree with that? Or what is your pronoun?
[00:25] And God said, in my imaginary conversation, God said, I told Moses what my pronouns are, because Moses in the Old Testament met God on the top of a mountain, and he asked, who are you? What's your name? And God said, I am the I am.
[00:45] So God's pronoun is I. Mine also.
