# Eckhart Tolle on the True Meaning of Love

[00:00] The Dalai Lama said, my religion is kindness. That's quite a surprising statement. He didn't say my religion is Buddhism. My religion is kindness. What is that kindness? It's loving kindness. Feel an outflow of loving kindness towards the other person. And what you can sense then is beyond that person, there is their being, their consciousness, which is one with your consciousness. You can sense the unconditioned in the other. This is the end of the delusion of absolute separateness. And that's love. That's the true meaning of love.
